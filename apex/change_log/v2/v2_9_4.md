# Apex 2.9.4

The action inbox stops being open to everyone, and the driver app reads its trips through the same identity it writes them with.

- **The action inbox is for desk users only.** The page named no roles, and a page that names none is open to everyone rather than closed to everyone — its definition could even be fetched without signing in. It is now limited to users who have desk access. Portal drivers and workers never opened it and lose nothing.
- **The driver app reads its trips under its own identity.** Today's trips and recent trips were read with the permission layer switched off. They now read through it, under the same portal identity that already records the driver's work, so what the app shows and what the app may write agree.
- **The portal identities were granted the reads they were already making.** Eight record types the driver and worker apps read now carry an explicit permission for those apps, instead of the reads going around the check.
- **Nothing already recorded is changed.** No trip, request, custody row or permission a person holds was altered.
