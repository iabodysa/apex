# Apex 2.0.0

A generational release: run workforce attendance timesheets end to end, manage the accommodation and logistics billing cycle, find your daily work faster on clearer, plainer-named screens, and rely on stronger safeguards throughout.

- **Workforce timesheets, end to end.** A new Logistay workspace brings client attendance into the app. Upload each client's and supervisor's attendance files, and they are read automatically every day into one standard set of attendance records — each client's own wording for present, absent, or off is mapped onto a single common code, with full traceability back to the source. Anything unclear or conflicting is raised as a tracked exception rather than silently dropped or overwritten.
- **An exception workbench for attendance.** Reviewers get a single report that groups every attendance data issue by type, entity, period, and field, with a governed way to waive — with a required reason — or resolve each group once, leaving a clear trail. Auditors keep a read-only view.
- **Canonical workforce records.** New Worker, Client, Branch Site, and Timesheet Period records give attendance stable, named records to attach to instead of loose text, and a single Worker identity now unifies temporary workers, freelancers, and employees.
- **Billing, invoicing, and payroll for Logistay.** The module now carries the full accommodation and logistics billing cycle: rate cards resolve the agreed price, reconciliation settles what is billable to clients and payable to workers, invoices are assembled with ZATCA-shaped e-invoicing, and payroll is prepared for WPS release. Each step passes through a governance layer of delegated-authority bands, separation-of-duties approvals, and an append-only audit trail, with an export safeguard before anything leaves the system. Your own rates, thresholds, tax, and bank details are configured per site.
- **Clearer names across the app.** Dozens of buildings, reports, fields, dashboards, and cards were renamed to shorter, plainer terms — the housing building record is simply Building now, reports and charts read consistently, and duplicate tiles were removed. Your existing records, links, and building-based access all carry over unchanged; only the displayed names are shorter.
- **Task-first daily workspaces.** Every operational role — resident supervisor, fleet supervisor, safety officer, and maintenance technician — now lands on its own daily home that opens on that role's real work: quick actions to create the day's records, key metrics, and a short guide, with boards and reports grouped below. This is a one-time default and never overrides a landing page you chose for yourself.
- **Expiry alerts that reach you directly.** Building-license, building-lease, and temporary-stay check-out reminders now arrive as email and in-app notifications to the responsible supervisor and manager, instead of resting as internal timeline notes.
- **Stronger safeguards, one steady look.** Each worker's personal Masar link is now kept as a secure one-way fingerprint plus a locked, encrypted copy, and its button now issues a fresh link and retires the old one on demand. The staff worker-link dialog safely escapes both the link and its QR image, so a crafted value can no longer slip markup into it. The five worker and driver portals share one foundation, so language switching and the Growth-Green look render exactly as before, and trip rating from the Masar portal now works correctly.
- **Smoother and quicker in daily use.** Marking a very large batch of resident requests as triaged now runs in the background, so the screen responds at once while smaller selections still complete right away. Worker boarding and dispatch lookups are quicker, shared helpers stand behind the portals, and the driver app no longer does a stray refresh on first load.
