# Apex 2.10.1

Seven housing and safety screens recover from a failed load, two portal buttons meet the app's own touch target, and the safety portal reads its own name.

- **Seven housing and safety portal pages recover from a failed load.** Each used to show a bare error line with no way back; it now shows the app's own error screen with a button to try again.
- **The custody page says what went wrong instead of drawing an empty list.** A failed balance fetch used to look identical to an empty one; it now shows the failure, or says the list is loading, or says it is empty, whichever is true.
- **The transport map shows an empty message when a filter matches nothing.** It used to leave a blank grid on screen instead.
- **Two worker portal buttons are easier to tap.** The stop and call actions now reach the same minimum touch size as the rest of the app.
- **The safety portal has its own name in the browser tab.** It used to show the housing app's name.
- **Nothing already recorded is changed.** No trip, request or custody record was altered.
