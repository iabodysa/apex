# Apex 2.1.1

A focused housekeeping release: SIM and telecom work now lives alongside the rest of custody, damaged-item costs are recovered through the standard advance, and the app is steadier to maintain.

## What changed for users

- **SIM and telecom moved in with Custody.** SIM cards, telecom contracts, and their reports and metrics now sit inside the Custody workspace instead of a separate area, so managing a worker's SIM is in the same place as the rest of their custody.
- **Recover damage costs through Employee Advance.** When an item is damaged, the amount owed is now recovered using the standard Employee Advance, so the deduction follows the normal, auditable advance route.
- **Steadier to maintain.** Internal safeguards were tightened so future updates stay reliable, with no change to your data or daily screens.
- **Your records carry over.** Existing SIM cards, custody records, and their links are unchanged; only where you find SIM management has moved.

## If you are updating an existing site

1. Run `bench --site <site> migrate`.
2. Run `bench build --app apex`.
3. Clear the site's cache before operators continue.
