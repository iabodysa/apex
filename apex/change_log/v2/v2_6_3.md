# Apex 2.6.3

Security and reliability improvements.

- Overdue temporary fuel top-ups are now reverted on schedule, and the go-live tours reach the supervisors whose daily work they teach.
- Nothing you could do before has been taken away, and the trip start record stays the machine-written audit record it has always been.
- Top-ups that were already past due are picked up on the next run; no past record is rewritten.
