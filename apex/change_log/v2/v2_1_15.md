# Apex 2.1.15

Five portals move onto one shared design, two housing reports ask a single scope authority, and the demo seeder now explains why it skipped.

- The housing, worker, driver, safety and fleet portals now share one design, so dark mode no longer leaves panels white and four buttons that rendered nothing now show their icons.
- The fleet request form drops a Save as draft button that saved nothing, and a rejected request keeps its reason on the form instead of flashing it away.
- Two housing reports now ask the one place that decides which buildings a supervisor may see, so a scope correction reaches all of them at once.
- Running the demo seeder on a fresh site now says why it did nothing: the site has no company until the setup wizard creates one.
- The app identity upgrade runbook opens by saying the cutover is deferred, names both blockers where the operator meets them, and corrects four commands that were wrong.
- Internal safeguards were widened: every place the app rolls back is now checked instead of a hand-kept list of seven, and the fleet navigation highlight is proven by mounting the page. Nothing changes on screen.
