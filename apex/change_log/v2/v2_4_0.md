# Apex 2.4.0

- Every document Apex prints was rebuilt: the paper names both parties, itemises what it covers with a total, states the checks it passed or failed, and carries the signature that binds someone
- Documents print in Arabic with an English sub-label under every heading and column
- Each printed page carries a QR that opens the record it came from, and the letterhead and page number repeat on every page
- Contracts state their VAT, their terms and the person who signs them, and a subcontractor order can be priced line by line
- A confirmed visit, a closed incident and a reviewed safety round record who signed them off and when
- Fleet documents carry the other party in an accident, what the station actually dispensed, and the itemised custody a departing driver returned
- A custody receipt freezes the value the worker signs for, and a housing clearance separates a custody release from a full one
- Installing Apex on a new site works again
