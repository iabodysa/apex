# Apex 2.0.1

Simpler Logistay records, consistent task-focused portals, fleet employee self-service, and passwordless barcode access for drivers and workers.

- **A leaner Logistay.** The workspace now focuses on the two workforce records operators use directly: Freelancer and Temporary Worker. The retired first-generation billing engine is archived behind a safe migration instead of crowding daily work.
- **One consistent portal system.** Driver and worker mobile consoles, housing and safety supervisor tablets, and fleet work screens now share three purpose-built layouts and one Growth Green visual foundation. Language switching and progressive-web-app behaviour stay consistent across them.
- **Fleet self-service without losing operations control.** Employees now open a personal My Fleet page for their assigned vehicle, fuel requests, and dispatch work. The full supervisor board remains available separately as Fleet OS.
- **Passwordless barcode entry for drivers and workers.** A driver can scan a personal QR and enter the same mobile portal foundation as a worker without needing a Frappe User account. The available actions change with the token holder type, and driver clearance revokes the driver's entry token automatically.
- **Safer credential cutover.** Personal links are stored as a one-way fingerprint plus a site-encrypted copy, expire after six months, and rotate when a revoked driver is re-issued. A legacy Website User is disabled only after that driver's barcode is live; System Users and staff with elevated operational roles are never disabled.
- **Clearer operational language.** Shorter record names, consistent suspension terms, and added Arabic translations make fleet and portal screens easier to scan.

## Under the hood

- The driver and worker portal now share one token model while keeping separate cookies and holder-type checks.
- The retired standalone driver frontend is no longer installed or checked as a separate build target.
- Driver-token naming no longer writes a driver identifier into the worker-party field.

## If updating an existing site

1. Run `bench --site <site> migrate` to apply the token-model and account-cutover changes.
2. Run `bench build --app apex` to publish the consolidated portal bundles.
3. Clear the site's cache before operators reopen the portals.
