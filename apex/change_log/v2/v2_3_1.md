# Apex 2.3.1

Forms and the Logistay page show what they promise.

- **Fewer, fuller tabs** — the tabs that opened onto nothing but a series number or an amended-from link are gone, and those fields now sit in a collapsed section at the end of the form.
- **SIMs in custody, counted where you work** — the Logistay page shows how many SIMs are held right now and opens that list already filtered, in place of the report that only listed them.
- Nothing you have recorded changes, and everyone keeps the access they had.
