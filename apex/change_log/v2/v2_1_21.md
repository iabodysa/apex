# Apex 2.1.21

Typing a transport request no longer disappears while the screen refreshes, safety verdicts survive a reload, photos are refused before upload if the server would reject them, and refusals now arrive in the language of the page.

- Filling in a transport request on the worker portal and pausing for a moment used to clear every field you had typed. The page no longer resets itself while it refreshes in the background, and a button pressed during a refresh is no longer ignored.
- Safety verdicts marked during a round looked saved but were lost on reload. They are now kept per building and day until you submit the round, and the screen says they are a draft.
- Attaching a photo that is not a JPEG, PNG or WebP is refused straight away, with the reason, instead of being reported as attached and then rejected by the server.
- A refusal raised on an Arabic screen now arrives in Arabic. Every portal sends the language of the page it came from, and your saved account language is not changed.
- On the route supervisor board the trip status reads in the language of the page instead of showing the stored English value inside an Arabic sentence.
- The driver home tiles each keep one colour identity instead of swapping between near-white and near-black with the light and dark schemes; one of them was also hard to read in dark mode.
- A scheduled boarding sweep no longer loses the whole run when a single trip cannot be confirmed.
