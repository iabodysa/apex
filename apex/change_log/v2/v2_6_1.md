# Apex 2.6.1

Custody now returns on the books as well as on paper, and each fleet list shows only the projects you work on.

- A resident's check-out now returns what they handed back into the building store, and records what was lost or damaged as gone. The store balance stays true, so a store that has stock no longer refuses to issue it.
- Two front-desk staff checking in at the same moment can no longer both take the same bed; the second is told the bed was just taken.
- Confirming a handover or a delivery now stops accepting codes after repeated wrong attempts, and waits a few minutes before allowing another.
- Cancelling or deleting a trip now frees its transport requests, so they can be put on a new trip instead of staying tied to a trip that no longer runs.
- Vehicle handovers, wash requests, daily fuel logs, rental movements and cost recoveries now show only the projects you are assigned to, in the list and over the API alike.
