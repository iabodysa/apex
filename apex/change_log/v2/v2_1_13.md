# Apex 2.1.13

A damage deduction can no longer clear a worker's advance on paper while the money is still owed on the books

- When a damage recovery is set up with a deduction that posts to a different account than the advance was paid from, the setting is now refused as you save it, with both accounts named.
- The refusal happens while configuring, never during a payroll run, so no worker's recovery is stopped mid-period.
- The fleet navigation on a narrow screen is covered by a check that matches how it now behaves.
