# Apex 2.8.0

Apex now has its own tile on the app screen, and the alerts that warn you before something lapses reach the people who act on them.

- **Apex opens from the app screen.** The screen lists a tile for the Apex desk alongside the portals, and every tile carries its own name, so the worker portal and the driver portal are no longer two entries reading the same.
- **A supervisor reads the inspections taken on his building.** The building supervisor, the maintenance technician and the accommodation manager can open a maintenance inspection report; a supervisor sees the buildings he holds and no others.
- **An expiring telecom contract warns you like an expiring licence.** It joins the vehicle compliance and building licence alerts, and a rent payment that falls due now warns a week ahead instead of on the day.
- **Switching email off switches it off.** Turning the email setting off stops every alert this app would send, and the setting says exactly that on the screen, including that other apps on the site keep their own email.
- **Money reads in your own currency.** Cards, charts and alerts take the currency from the site instead of showing a fixed one.
- **A new site opens its portals.** A freshly installed site reaches the worker and driver portals and the rest without a manual step first.
