# Apex 2.2.2

The portals show a live map, one denial screen, and clearer empty cards.

- **A map that draws** — the live driver map appears as soon as you open the tab, with the waiting note over it, instead of a blank box until the driver shares a position.
- **One refusal screen** — a page you cannot open explains itself in Arabic, in one style across the portals.
- **Clearer empty cards** — an empty vehicle or trips card carries an icon and reads in one numeral system with the date beside it.
- Nothing you have recorded changes, and everyone keeps the access they had.
