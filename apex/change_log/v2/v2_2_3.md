# Apex 2.2.3

The route supervisor screen can be linked to, and it fits the device you open it on.

- **Send a link to a plan** — the address bar now names the plan and the section you are looking at, so a link opens exactly that screen and the back button walks back through it.
- **A phone shows one thing at a time** — the plan list is the landing screen and a plan opens as its own screen with a back control, instead of both stacked in one long page.
- **A wide screen shows more** — from 1280 pixels the live driver map keeps a column of its own beside the list and the plan, rather than hiding behind a tab.
- Nothing you have recorded changes, and everyone keeps the access they had.
