# Apex 2.3.2

The desk reads Arabic wherever Apex writes the words.

- **Arabic section headings** — five headings that stayed English now read Arabic with the rest of the form they head.
- **How to switch the desk** — the installation guide gives the one setting that puts the desk in Arabic, and names the two surfaces that stay English: the workspaces other applications own, and chart labels, which print the stored value.
- Nothing you have recorded changes, and everyone keeps the access they had.
