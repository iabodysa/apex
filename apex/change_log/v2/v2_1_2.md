# Version 2.1.2

## What changed

- A user holding only the SIM Operations role sees their surface in the sidebar again, and the two Salis compliance personas — Government Relations Officer and Internal Auditor — reach the Salis branch instead of a blank sidebar.
- The bed-occupancy indexes now reach sites that were installed before the fix, so the housing occupancy lookup stops scanning the whole table there.
- The Fleet employee page navigation works: each link moves you to its section and the highlight follows the section you are actually reading, at both desktop and phone widths, in Arabic and English.
- The supervisor map and every portal typeface are served from the app itself instead of public content delivery networks — the portals now load fully offline and pull in no unpinned third-party code.
- A nightly cleanup reclaims oversized access-log rows that age-based retention cannot see, with a dry run, bounded batches and a configurable threshold.
- Published documentation now describes the seven portal routes the app actually serves, with the audience and sign-in path for each.
- Security and reliability: the portal smoke check now runs on every push instead of never, the bundle staleness guard catches a new file as well as a changed one, and several test suites that were quietly skipping themselves now build their own data and genuinely run.
