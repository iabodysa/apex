# Apex 2.7.0

Your fleet screens now keep a stopped driver or vehicle out of tomorrow's work.

- **Stopping a driver is safe.** You cannot stop a driver while trips are still assigned to him — the message names each trip so you can hand it over first. A trip already on the road never blocks you; it is recorded on the stop instead.
- **Pickers offer only what can work.** Choosing a driver or a vehicle for a trip, a route, a fuel request or a handover now lists only the active ones. Records that are about a stopped driver or vehicle — a clearance, an incident, a claim — still let you pick them.
- **A recurring route keeps running.** When a driver is stopped, the route's next trips are created without him and wait in the assignment queue, so the workers on that route are not left without transport.
- **The supervisor closes his own trips.** The supervisor who dispatched a trip can now complete it, so a finished trip leaves his board instead of waiting for a manager.
- **Today's trip board can be opened without signing in.** It shows the route, the plate and the status, and names no rider.
- **A salary deduction says why it did nothing.** Pressing deduct while recovery is switched off now tells you it is switched off, instead of appearing to work.
