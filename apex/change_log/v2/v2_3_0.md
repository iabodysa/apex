# Apex 2.3.0

- Fourteen reports retired into the list views, sibling reports and workspace cards that already answered the same question
- Every surviving report opens with filters and a strip of figures at the top
- Nine new registers: vehicle assignment and handover, custody issue and return, goods receipt, resident requests, passenger manifests, room and bed transfers, safety incidents
- Alerts are gone: an overdue vehicle, driver or quota is now assigned to the supervisor who can act, and the assignment closes itself when the condition clears
- Twelve documents that leave the premises now print on headed A4 with signature blocks
- Forms show their related records: thirty document types gained the connections their operators were missing
- The fleet portal navigation now opens real pages for the vehicle, the trips and the fuel request
- All eight portals share one look, with keyboard focus and touch targets fixed throughout
- The supervisor board loads in a fixed six queries however many route plans it shows
- The demo now fills every register, so an empty screen means empty, not unfinished
