# Apex 2.3.0

The supervisor portal gets its own screens: every driver on one map, and approvals in one place.

- **Every driver on one map** — a new screen shows all the drivers on your plans at once, with filters by driver and by plan, instead of one driver at a time.
- **The route on the road** — a plan is drawn along the streets it actually uses, with its stops numbered, so a bus that is off route is visible at a glance.
- **Approvals in one place** — the plans waiting for your decision have their own screen, each with approve and reject on the row.
- Nothing you have recorded changes, and everyone keeps the access they had.
