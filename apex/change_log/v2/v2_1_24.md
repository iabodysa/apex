# Apex 2.1.24

The setup wizard and the worker and driver link screens now read in Arabic instead of falling back to English.

- Every page of the setup wizard now reads in Arabic: the company and accounting defaults, the notifications and operations page, and the salary deductions page, along with each checkbox and the sentence under it that explains what leaving it off means.
- The worker's Masar link and the driver portal link screens now read in Arabic too, including the copy and WhatsApp actions, the expiry line, and the warning that anyone holding the link or the barcode enters as that person.
- Nothing else changes on screen. Two test modules that graded the repository's own translation policy rather than the application's behaviour were removed from the shipped tests, and the check that finds an untranslated screen now looks in the one place it had never been looking.
