# Apex 2.10.0

The fuel and wash platforms become one screen, four reports answer what a unit actually costs, and a hundred and ninety Arabic lines stop saying the wrong thing.

- **Fuel Platform and Wash Platform are one Service Platform.** The two lists held the same four fields and forced a choice that meant nothing. They are now one screen with a service type, every existing row carried over, and the Fleet workspace points at the one that is left.
- **Four reports say what a unit costs.** Cost per employee, per vehicle, per driver, and a Logistay manpower roster. Until now the report list restated rows and never totalled a cost.
- **A hundred and ninety Arabic lines were corrected.** The worst told you a document was approved while it was still waiting; the same word now means one thing across the app, and write-off no longer reads as destruction.
- **The action inbox is for desk users only.** It named no roles, and a page that names none is open to everyone rather than closed to everyone.
- **The driver app's trip lists are back.** They had gone empty; they list trips again.
- **Records that were built in code are now declared.** Role profiles and the Admin Manager role ship as data, an orphaned-card sweep runs once as a patch instead of on every migrate, and a database index stops being rebuilt on every upgrade.
- **Nothing already recorded was changed.** No trip, request, custody row, platform record or permission a person holds was altered.
