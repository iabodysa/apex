# Apex 2.1.12

The portals share one set of colours, one keyboard outline and one theme switch

- Two portals kept their own copy of the colours, so a colour corrected centrally never reached them. They now read the shared set, in light and dark alike.
- Several colours were too faint against the text placed on them and have been corrected; two status colours had quietly become identical.
- The keyboard outline is back on every portal, and the automatic theme setting no longer clears the theme your site chose.
- On a narrow screen the fleet navigation wraps onto a second row instead of disappearing.
