# Apex 2.9.0

Telecom gets its own settings screen with the expiry warning set to your number, and what an approval locks now stays locked.

- **Telecom has its own settings screen.** You choose how many days ahead an expiring contract warns you, instead of living with a number fixed inside the app.
- **An approval locks what it approved.** Twenty-four fields across housing and fleet stop accepting an edit once the record is approved, so a figure cannot move behind a decision already taken. Nothing already approved is rewritten, and no approval you hold changes hands.
- **A damage recovery is raised only when something stands behind it.** On a site that does not run lending, a vehicle incident saves as it should and no recovery is raised against a driver on nothing. No recovery raised earlier is altered.
- **A trip rating shows the rating it was given.** Every rating drew as five stars whatever the driver actually scored; each one now shows its own score.
- **The transport request names its building.** The request screen shows the building a trip starts from, where the name used to stay blank.
- **A list shows you all of it.** Front desk, arrivals, safety, telecom and fleet lists stopped silently at twenty rows and now show every row you are allowed to see — and each screen reads records through your own permissions, so what you see is what is yours.
- **Your personal task screen opens for you.** The personal task workspace had narrowed to administrators; everyone who holds tasks reaches it again.
- **A smaller paycheque takes a smaller installment.** A loan installment now fits the pay of the period it falls in, so a month with less pay no longer loses most of it to an amount fixed earlier. Past payroll is not recalculated.
- **A repeated click cannot pay twice.** A payment request and a trip log each complete once, even when two people act at the same moment.
- **Daily scans stop repeating themselves.** The fleet and maintenance queues comment when a document is newly assigned to you, not once more every day its condition still holds.
- **More of the screen reads in Arabic.** The vehicle incident loan fields, the maintenance work stage and the scheduled safety alerts now arrive translated.
