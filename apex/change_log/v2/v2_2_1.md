# Apex 2.2.1

Corrections to what you see and who can see it.

- **Appearance** — the portal appearance screen offers the two themes that exist, so the one you pick always changes the screen.
- **Your own attendance** — a driver sees the attendance they recorded in the attendance summary, the same rows the list already showed them.
- **Tighter access** — a permission granted for one kind of record no longer widens what its holder sees in the others.
- Nothing you have already recorded changes, and everyone keeps the access they had before.
