# Apex 2.2.0

The desk opens on a single Apex page that lists the portals, with five module workspaces beneath it instead of nine unrelated ones.

- A new Apex page is the landing screen: it carries a link to each of the eight portals and nothing else.
- Nine workspaces became five. Salis is one page again with a Masar card for worker movement, and Habitat's four pages became Housing and Safety and Custody and Costs.
- Fourteen record types were reachable from no workspace at all, including every telecom record. All of them now have a home, and the telecom ones moved to Logistay, which gained the workspace its module never had.
- Every go-live checklist was named after a page that no longer exists. There are now six, one per page you can open, including a Logistay checklist that did not exist before.
- Forty-one checklist steps carried a paragraph of guidance no screen has ever shown, because the field it was written into does not exist. The text now appears where the step is described.
