# Apex 2.1.14

The Arabic across the app now reads the way a Saudi employee speaks

- Ninety labels and messages moved from formal or borrowed wording to the plain word, and several that said the wrong thing were corrected: one control told a driver to take a vehicle out where the screen releases it.
- Operational records are called records and the accounting term is kept for the ledger, so the two stop being confused on screen.
- Wording shared with the HR and ERP apps was left alone, because one translation row serves every app on the site.
