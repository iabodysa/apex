# Apex 2.9.2

Settings for stock and integration now sit inside the module screens that own them, and a set of security and reliability repairs.

- **Two settings screens moved into the ones beside them.** What you set for the building store now lives in Habitat Settings, and what you set for the external frontend and the messaging gateway now lives in Salis Settings. Every value you had set moved with them, including anything you had deliberately switched off. Nothing needs re-entering.
- **A portal account sees only its own device registrations.** The list of registered notification devices was readable in full from the portal side; it is closed there now. No registration changed and nobody needs to sign in again.
- **A new site starts with the portal accounts closed.** On a site installed from scratch, the shared portal accounts could reach the back office until the first update ran. They are closed from the moment the site is installed.
- **Worker links send again.** After the settings move, the message that carries a worker his personal link had stopped going out. It works.
- **A damage recovery is priced by the loan itself.** The installment shown on a payslip comes from the recovery's own repayment schedule, and a second company can now raise one where it previously failed.
- **Nothing already recorded is changed.** Existing recoveries, registrations and settings values keep what they had.
