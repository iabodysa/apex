# Apex 2.9.3

Wage recoveries take the share you set, the driver app carries its own name, and the housing screen drops a switch nothing could turn on.

- **A recovery takes the share you set.** Where you had narrowed the share of a wage that a recovery installment may take, the installment still took the legal maximum of half the gross pay. It now takes yours. A recovery already running follows the narrower share from the next payslip; the total owed is unchanged.
- **The driver app carries its own name and icon.** On a phone holding both apps, the driver app wore the worker app's icon, name and notification title. Each app now shows its own.
- **The housing screen drops a switch nothing could turn on.** A suspension checkbox on a housing assignment could never be set from anywhere; it and the near-empty tab around it are gone. No assignment changed.
- **Nothing already recorded is changed.** Existing recoveries, assignments and settings values keep what they had.
