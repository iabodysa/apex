# Apex 2.1.19

Housing records can be created from the form again, portal dialogs keep the keyboard inside them, and a refused safety round now says why.

- A Housing Assignment could not be created from the form at all: picking a building, room or bed silently cleared the value you had just picked, and Save then asked for five fields you had already filled. The form no longer clears what it just accepted.
- The Arrivals Desk opened to a permission dialog for the very people it is meant for, and the Active Resident Register hung on Loading as soon as there was one resident to show. Both now open for the housing supervisor.
- In the route supervisor screens, pressing Tab inside a dialog or the mobile menu used to walk out of it onto controls hidden behind the dimmed background, and Escape did not always close it. The keyboard now stays inside, Escape closes, and focus returns to the button that opened it.
- Submitting a safety round that needs a photo was refused by the server and the screen said nothing at all. The refusal now appears on the form, under the button, and names the task and the missing photo.
- The driver portal was rendering Arabic in a substitute typeface because it never loaded the Arabic font the app ships, and a bad driver link showed a raw internal error string instead of a message. Both are fixed.
- Nothing changes on screen from the rest of this batch: about two thousand lines of tests that only checked the shape of the source were removed, each replaced by a named rule or kept where no rule could do the job.
