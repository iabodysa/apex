# Apex Habitat 0.8

You can now bill suppliers back for what their workers cost, watch occupancy trends over time, and print a signed handover in seconds.

## 0.8.6

_A new Front Desk board lets you check residents in and out by colour, and idle workers now carry a running cost you can see at a glance._

- **Run the front desk by colour** — a new visual board walks building to floor to room to bed. Beds are colour-coded: green is available and ready, red is occupied and shows the resident, amber is available but the room isn't ready, and grey is out of service.
- **Check in with a tap** — tapping a green bed opens a short dialog for employee, project, and date, and creates a real accommodation assignment, so every check runs as normal.
- **Check out with a tap** — tapping an occupied bed vacates it in one step; if the resident still holds custody items, it opens the full check-out form instead so nothing is lost.
- **Let supervisors work the board** — resident supervisors can now check residents in and out straight from the Front Desk.
- **Never pay for a forgotten worker** — flagging an idle resident now raises a follow-up for the responsible team, and a daily count shows the days idle and an estimated accommodation cost so the bleed stays in plain sight.

After updating, the new Front Desk appears in your sidebar.

## 0.8.5

_You can now book short stays with a clear end date and start holding idle workers to account._

- **Short stays that stay short** — an accommodation assignment now carries a stay type, Permanent or Temporary, and for a temporary stay an expected check-out date, so a short stay never quietly becomes permanent.
- **Catch a stay before it overruns** — a daily watchlist flags temporary residents nearing their check-out date and warns when someone is still checked in past it, with an opt-in email reminder a couple of days ahead. The reminder is off by default, so no email goes out until you turn it on.
- **Spotlight idle workers** — a new tracker for a worker living in camp but not yet deployed. You capture the employee, building, reason, and the responsible department, then move it from Open to Acknowledged to Resolved.
- **A clear status flow** — every idle-worker record carries its own state, so it stays visible until someone closes it out.

## 0.8.4

_The right reports, reminders, and follow-ups can now send themselves on schedule, so less slips through the cracks._

- **Reports that send themselves** — supplier cost recovery monthly, plus occupancy trend, maintenance backlog, and scheduled task compliance weekly, can land in inboxes on schedule. They ship switched off, so nothing is emailed until you set your real recipients and turn it on.
- **Polished emails in one click** — ready-made templates for building licence renewal, lease renewal, and resident-request acknowledgement, right from the Email action on those documents.
- **Nothing slips through the cracks** — assigning a resident request drops a follow-up into that person's queue, and resolving or closing the request ticks it off automatically.
- **Your key workspaces one click away** — the Help menu now jumps straight to the Command Center and Setup.

## 0.8.3

_You can now see your work on a calendar, move rooms across a board, and let new requests route themselves to a team._

- **See what's due on a calendar** — scheduled tasks by due date, building licences by expiry, service orders by scheduled date, and leases from start to end, all laid out by date.
- **Turn rooms around faster** — a housekeeping board lets you drag each room from Needs Cleaning to Needs Repair to Ready, alongside the resident and maintenance boards.
- **Let requests route themselves** — new maintenance and resident requests can auto-assign to a team, sharing the load evenly or in turn. This ships switched off, so nothing is assigned until you add your real team and turn it on.
- **Keep your team in the loop** — opt-in email alerts for a maintenance request assigned to you, a new request awaiting triage, a resident request waiting on evidence, a new resident request, and a submitted custody damage assessment. These start switched off, so no email goes out until you enable it.

## 0.8.2

_Every building now works like its own store, so you always know what supplies you hold and where they went._

- **Each building holds its own stock** — issuing a custody item to an employee moves it out of the building and into that person's hands, and a return moves it back. Every move is tracked, so you can always see what's where.
- **Move supplies between buildings safely** — a new transfer ships custody articles and maintenance materials from one building to another: it leaves as In Transit, then you mark it received at the destination. You can never send more than a building actually holds.
- **Keep finance informed on cross-charges** — when stock moves between buildings on different cost centres, finance can be sent a heads-up memo to record the cross-charge. This is off by default and never touches a ledger on its own.
- **See what you have and what it's worth** — a new stock balance report shows on-hand quantity and value for every building and every employee's custody, from the Custody and Asset Control workspace.
- **Triage by dragging** — resident requests and maintenance requests now have drag-to-progress boards for daily work.

## 0.8.1

_A new dashboard on the building and supplier forms gives you a live picture of each one the moment you open it._

- **See a building's health on open.** An accommodation building now greets you at the top of the form with an occupancy-percent trend chart and live counts for active occupants, open maintenance and open custody issues, alongside its rooms, leases, licences and occupancy snapshots.
- **See a supplier's footprint on open.** A supplier now shows their housing assignments, leases, subcontracting orders and cost-recovery entries side by side with the standard purchase orders and invoices.
- **Get the per-record view, not just the workspace.** You no longer have to leave the form to understand where a single building or supplier stands.

## 0.8.0

_You can now bill suppliers back for what their workers cost, watch occupancy trends over time, and print a signed handover in seconds._

- **Recover what suppliers' workers cost you.** Tag an accommodation assignment as an external supplier worker, and the new Supplier Cost Recovery report totals each supplier's monthly cost and adds your markup — ready to bill. The markup is off until you turn it on in Settings, and it never touches a ledger on its own.
- **See how full your buildings have been.** Buildings keep a daily occupancy snapshot, and the new Occupancy Trend report charts the lows, averages, highs and days over capacity across any period you choose.
- **Print a signed handover in seconds.** Five ready-to-sign documents are built in — the custody issue and return receipts, a damage and deduction notice, the checkout clearance, and the maintenance work-order job sheet — each with signature blocks.
- **Get alerts where you work.** Licence and lease expiry and overdue tasks now post on the document's own timeline and, if you choose, in your inbox — all controlled by a single Operational Notifications switch you can turn off any time.
- **Read the operation at a glance.** New cards and charts across the workspaces show cost this month, supplier-billed cost, assets moved, tasks due and completed, and occupancy and cost trends.
- **Keep your records clean.** Apex now warns you before you enter a duplicate utility bill, an overlapping lease, a duplicate work order, or a custody return for more than was issued.
