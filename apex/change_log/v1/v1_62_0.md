# Apex Habitat 1.62.0

Confirm asset deliveries from your phone, rate your trips, and find your daily work faster on clearer workspaces.

- The Housing Portal now has an Asset Delivery screen: once an asset's three exit checkpoints - Security, Logistics, and Receiving - are cleared, the receiving supervisor confirms the transfer on-site by entering the 6-digit code from their phone.
- Workers can now rate a completed trip from the Masar portal, giving a 1-to-5 star rating with optional feedback that is recorded against the trip.
- Every operational workspace now follows a task-first layout - Daily Tasks first, then Key Reports, then Master Data last - so everyday work sits right at hand.
- Workspace cards now show what each screen is - an input screen, a report, or a monitoring board - with a plain-language type legend and a type label on links that could be mistaken.
- New staff get a step-by-step guided tour the first time they open the busiest forms: Accommodation Assignment, Custody Issue, Maintenance Request, Transport Request, and Fuel Request.
- Several Salis Settings help notes that were still showing in English now read in Arabic.
