# Apex Habitat 1.60.56

Security and reliability hardening: worker identity documents are scope-restricted, lookups are building-scoped, and night-shift trips stay visible across midnight.

- Worker identity documents are now released only to staff scoped to that worker's estate
- Arrival and check-in lookups are confined to the staff member's own buildings
- Night-shift trips that span midnight stay visible to the worker after 00:00
- Scheduler resilience and date-boundary fixes across custody, safety, and fleet alerts
