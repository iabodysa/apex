# Apex Habitat 1.60.1

Security hardening and reliability fixes across portals, fleet, custody and reports.

- Asset, custody, and driver records, and the reports built on them, stay scoped to your own organization.
- Concurrent stock issues, boarding scans, custody handovers, and fuel-quota allocations no longer collide or double-count.
- Driver and worker portals open reliably, and untrusted text on pages and printed slips can no longer run scripts.
- Background and billing jobs are dependable: deductions never apply twice, and utility bills reject negative or duplicate amounts.
- Nothing changes retroactively; existing records are unaffected.
