# Apex Habitat 1.60.48

Operational Desk boards now render with a clear, consistent visual style in both light and dark mode.

- Styled boards: the front desk, room setup, safety map, transfer board, work center, operations control, and dispatch board now show color-keyed cards, status pills, and grids instead of a plain list.
- Light and dark ready: every board follows your current theme colors and reads correctly in both left-to-right and right-to-left layouts.
- Steadier actions: alert, room-readiness, and search actions now report a clear message if something goes wrong instead of failing silently.
- Nothing changes in your data: this is a presentation and reliability update only; your records and permissions are untouched.
