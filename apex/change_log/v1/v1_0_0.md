# Apex Habitat 1.0 to 1.59

A consolidated look back at the 1.0 through 1.59 releases, the run that grew Apex from housing into a full housing-and-movement platform.

## What changed

- **A whole movement module, Salis.** Alongside Habitat you gained fleet, trips, fuel, rentals, and cost recovery, so you manage where your workforce lives and how it moves in one place.
- **Guided approval paths.** Transport requests, fuel requests and claims, rental settlements, building leases, utility bills, and deductions all move through clear, role-based approval steps before they take effect.
- **A driver app and the Masar worker app.** Drivers run their whole day from a phone — trips, fuel, check-ins, and document expiry dates — while workers open one personal page for their accommodation, transport, and documents, and can request a ride for themselves.
- **The Arrivals Desk and custody.** Onboard an arriving worker from one screen — bed, custody, arrival card, and transport — including workers who arrive before their Iqama, with custody issue, return, and damage tracked throughout.
- **Safety and alerts you can act on.** Log accommodation safety incidents in one place, with serious ones raising the alarm, and a fleet alert list you can take, assign, snooze, and clear.
- **My Work and the Action Inbox.** One home opens on what is waiting for you — approvals, tasks, notifications, and mentions — with Approve and Reject right there.
- **Reliability and Arabic coverage throughout.** Steady records across custody, fuel, rental, and maintenance, plus a fuller Arabic interface across the desk and portals.
