# Apex Habitat 1.60.8

Per-worker boarding audit, scoped operations alerts, financial-control guards, cleaner desk + portal UI.

- New per-worker Trip Boarding Ledger: one immutable, automatically posted row per worker's final boarding outcome on a finalized trip.
- Operations alerts are now scoped to a user's projects, custody issues are checked against store availability, and a role bundle was tightened.
- Financial-control guards: a rental settlement can no longer overpay beyond the reconciled amount, and a lease payment selects the correct row.
- Backend engines were removed from every workspace, and desk pages now use the native desk theme.
- Driver app gets a cleaner 2-tab navigation, a guarded fleet driver lens, and a worker 'driver on the way' indicator.
