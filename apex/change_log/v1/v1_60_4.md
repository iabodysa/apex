# Apex Habitat 1.60.4

You can now run a two-sided bus boarding and departure flow, with live confirmation between the driver and each rider.

- **Live boarding manifest.** The driver sees every rider's status and confirms or rejects each boarding as it happens.
- **Themed boarding pass.** A rider claims a seat on their phone and can ask the driver to wait a moment.
- **Countdown and wrong-bus help.** Riders get a countdown after each reminder, and a wrong scan shows the correct trip and driver.
- **Steadier reports.** Reports stay scoped to the buildings and fleet you may see; existing data is unaffected.
