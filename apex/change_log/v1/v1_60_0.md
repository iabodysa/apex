# Apex Habitat 1.60.0

Keep a running count of what's in each building, and let drivers get a push even when the app is closed.

- **Count every building.** A new housing inventory register tracks items per building and room, with expected against counted, the difference, and condition.
- **Spot shortfalls at a glance.** Each count flags the variance and items that need attention.
- **Maintenance keeps it honest.** Completing a work order updates the matching inventory row for you.
- **Drivers get a push with the app closed.** Opt in to background notifications so trip and fuel updates reach drivers even when the app is shut. Off until you turn it on in Settings.
