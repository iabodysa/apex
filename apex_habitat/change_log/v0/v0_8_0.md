# Apex 0.8.0

You can now bill suppliers back for what their workers cost, watch occupancy trends over time, and print a signed handover in seconds.


- **Recover what suppliers' workers cost you.** Tag an accommodation assignment as an external supplier worker, and the new Supplier Cost Recovery report totals each supplier's monthly cost and adds your markup — ready to bill. The markup is off until you turn it on in Settings, and it never touches a ledger on its own.
- **See how full your buildings have been.** Buildings keep a daily occupancy snapshot, and the new Occupancy Trend report charts the lows, averages, highs and days over capacity across any period you choose.
- **Print a signed handover in seconds.** Five ready-to-sign documents are built in — the custody issue and return receipts, a damage and deduction notice, the checkout clearance, and the maintenance work-order job sheet — each with signature blocks.
- **Get alerts where you work.** Licence and lease expiry and overdue tasks now post on the document's own timeline and, if you choose, in your inbox — all controlled by a single Operational Notifications switch you can turn off any time.
- **Read the operation at a glance.** New cards and charts across the workspaces show cost this month, supplier-billed cost, assets moved, tasks due and completed, and occupancy and cost trends.
- **Keep your records clean.** Apex now warns you before you enter a duplicate utility bill, an overlapping lease, a duplicate work order, or a custody return for more than was issued.
