# Apex 0.8.4

The right reports, reminders, and follow-ups can now send themselves on schedule, so less slips through the cracks.

## What changed

- **Reports that send themselves** — supplier cost recovery monthly, plus occupancy trend, maintenance backlog, and scheduled task compliance weekly, can land in inboxes on schedule. They ship switched off, so nothing is emailed until you set your real recipients and turn it on.
- **Polished emails in one click** — ready-made templates for building licence renewal, lease renewal, and resident-request acknowledgement, right from the Email action on those documents.
- **Nothing slips through the cracks** — assigning a resident request drops a follow-up into that person's queue, and resolving or closing the request ticks it off automatically.
- **Your key workspaces one click away** — the Help menu now jumps straight to the Command Center and Setup.
