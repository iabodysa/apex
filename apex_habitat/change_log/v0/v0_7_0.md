# Apex 0.7.0

You can now stand up a whole building in minutes, turn a lease into a payment in one click, and see operational warnings the moment they happen.

## What changed

- **Build a building floor by floor.** The room generator lays out floor after floor and lets you mix room types freely in one plan — worker, driver and supervisor rooms, offices, storage and maintenance rooms. Apartments set up the same way, with floor fields stepping aside when they do not apply.
- **Read room and bed numbers at a glance.** IDs now follow a floor-code numbering, so you can tell where a bed sits without opening it.
- **Catch operational warnings early.** Licence and lease reminders and other scheduled checks now surface as alerts right on the desk, where you will actually see them.
- **Log every safety finding in one place.** Each inspection report keeps its findings in a single tidy list.
- **Turn a lease into a payment in one click.** Raise the payment straight from the lease, with no re-keying.
- **Trust your cost figures.** Daily cost allocation now lands accurately every day of the year, so your housing, maintenance, safety and finance reports add up.
