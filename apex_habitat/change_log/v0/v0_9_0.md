# Apex Habitat 0.9

You can now run the busiest housing tasks from fast, tap-friendly screens instead of filling in forms.

## 0.9.1

_A new guided setup walks you through each workspace, and every record now shows what it is connected to._

- **Get started with step-by-step banners.** The Setup workspace walks you from settings to creating a site, building, rooms and beds, reviewing the safety catalog, and recording an assignment.
- **Go live on Accommodation in order.** A guided path takes you through opening the Front Desk, recording an assignment, and reviewing the Active Resident Register.
- **Check your safety readiness.** A short path has you review the safety catalog, log an inspection, and open the Safety Map.
- **See everything a record is linked to.** Open an Accommodation Building and its rooms, beds, assignments, requests, licences, custody, leases, utility and scheduling records are grouped in one Connections tab. Accommodation Site and Accommodation Room get the same drill-down.

After updating, turn on onboarding in your settings if you want the setup banners to appear.

## 0.9.0

_You can now run the busiest housing tasks from fast, tap-friendly screens instead of filling in forms._

- **Move a resident by dragging a bed.** The new Transfer Board shows two buildings side by side. Drag an occupied bed onto an empty one, or tap the source then the target on a tablet, and both sides repaint the moment the move is done.
- **Spot rooms that need attention on a floor plan.** The Safety Map lays out your rooms and common zones visually. Rooms that need a look pulse for you; tap one to log a safety inspection on the spot.
- **Issue custody from a checkout counter.** The Custody Kiosk gives storekeepers a big-tile screen: pick an employee, tap article images into a cart, and hit Issue.
- **Check in the right person, with photo proof.** Quick Check-in now shows the worker's profile photo so you confirm who you are checking in, and both check-in and check-out can attach a room-condition snapshot to settle any future damage dispute.
- **Read the heavy charts on one dashboard.** A Habitat dashboard now holds the Beds-by-Status chart, linked from the workspace, so the workspace stays a clean place to navigate from.
