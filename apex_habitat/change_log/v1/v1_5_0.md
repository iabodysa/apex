# Apex 1.5.0

Records connect across forms, Salis gets a guided start, and navigation is clearer.

## What changed

- **See related records on every form.** Open a vehicle, driver, custody article, maintenance request, or lease, and the Connections tab now shows the documents linked to it — no more empty list.
- **A guided start for Salis.** A new "Salis Go-Live" journey walks a new site through its first vehicle category, vehicle, driver, assignment, dispatch, and fuel request, right from the top of the Salis workspace.
- **Dashboards and charts within reach.** The Salis area workspaces now link straight to their dashboards, the main Salis page shows trend charts at a glance, and area names read short and clear in the sidebar.
- **Ready-to-enable email for the movement team.** Optional email templates (driver clearance, rental settlement, transport acknowledgement) and scheduled email reports (fuel reconciliation, cost-recovery aging, fulfilment, compliance) are available to switch on when you want them — off until you enable them.

After updating, the Salis workspace shows its new guide, dashboard links, and trend charts.
