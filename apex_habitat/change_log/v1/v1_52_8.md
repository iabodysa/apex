# Apex Habitat 1.52.8

App-wide form and workspace UX overhaul.

- Every DocType form is regrouped by concern: related fields sit together, audit and system fields move to their own tab or a collapsed section, and columns pack by field width.
- Workspaces overhauled — dashboard charts and live quick-lists wired in, report links given dedicated sections, shortcut tiles now show live counts, and every DocType is reachable from a workspace.
- Building room and floor counts now compute correctly (they were stuck at zero) and are backfilled on existing buildings; city and district auto-fill from the site.
- Hundreds of Arabic section labels added; toolbar buttons with two or fewer actions now show directly instead of under a menu.
