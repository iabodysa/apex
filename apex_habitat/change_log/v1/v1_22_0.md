# Apex Habitat 1.22

You can now dress the driver portal in a new dark theme, with a cleaner Frappe look alongside it.

## 1.22.1

_Reliability and polish across the front-desk and fleet console screens._

- **Clearer screens.** Front Desk, Custody Kiosk, Safety Map, Transfer Board, Fuel Approval, and Dispatch now show loading, empty, and retry states instead of a blank or stale view.
- **Your data stays correct.** Switching context no longer leaves an old result on screen, and action buttons no longer fire twice — amounts, approvals, and records are untouched.
- **Nothing changes for existing records.** These are screen-side improvements only; your saved data carries over exactly as it was.

## 1.22.0

_You can now dress the driver portal in a new dark theme, with a cleaner Frappe look alongside it._

- **A new dark theme for the driver portal.** Pick **Gemini** in the portal theme setting for a dark, glass-like look, alongside the existing **AFMCO**, **Frappe Standard**, and **Dark** choices. You can switch back at any time.
- **The Frappe Standard theme now truly looks like Frappe.** Frappe blue, the system font, softer shadows, a thin header, and denser list rows — clearly its own style rather than a recoloured AFMCO.
- **The worker route page loads again.** The page that had been erroring on open now shows correctly, and the Apex Core settings home is back to a complete, organised layout.
