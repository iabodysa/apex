# Apex 1.50.7

A reliability fix in resident check-out.

## What changed

- Checking the same resident out twice at the very same moment can no longer double-close their stay or duplicate the follow-up records.
- Nothing changes about how you record a check-out, or about the bed and room updates that follow.
- Existing check-outs are unaffected; the safeguard applies from now on.
