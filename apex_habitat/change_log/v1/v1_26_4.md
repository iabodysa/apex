# Apex 1.26.4

A tidier sidebar with fewer, more logical workspaces.


- **Facilities in one place.** Maintenance and Safety now share a single Facilities workspace, since they are the same daily upkeep surface.
- **Movement in one place.** Transport and Fuel now share a single Movement workspace, matching how you actually work a dispatch and the fuel behind it.
- **Nothing else moved.** Fleet stays its own surface, and every report, page and record stays exactly where it was.
