# Apex Habitat 1.60.60

Workers see a live ETA for their next shuttle: the driver app shares its GPS position while a trip is under way, and the worker's Home shows how many minutes until the ride reaches their pickup

- The driver app streams its live GPS position (opt-in, only while a trip is dispatched) so the ride can be tracked
- The worker's Home next-ride card now shows a server-computed ETA in minutes, from the driver's position to the worker's own pickup building
- Housing buildings can carry pickup coordinates, and a tunable assumed fleet speed drives the estimate
