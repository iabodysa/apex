# Apex Habitat 1.44

Reliability improvements in operational logs and snapshots.

## 1.44.0

_Reliability improvements in operational logs and snapshots._

- **Operational lists stay lean.** Alert and occupancy/utilisation snapshot lists now age out old entries on their own, so the desk stays fast over time.
- **Your financial records are never touched.** Accommodation, fuel and rental cost records are kept in full and are not part of this clean-up.
- **Existing data is safe.** This applies going forward only; nothing already recorded is changed or removed early.
