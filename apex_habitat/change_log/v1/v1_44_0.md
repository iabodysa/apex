# Apex 1.44.0

Growth control from the KB-grounded audit (Phase 3, Log Settings).

## What changed for users

- **Three operational logs now self-clean.** Operations Alerts and the two
  utilisation/occupancy snapshot tables grew without bound (a daily/weekly job
  kept adding rows and nothing ever removed them). They are now registered with
  Frappe's Log Settings so the daily maintenance job ages out old rows
  automatically. Financial ledgers are deliberately left untouched.

## What changed under the hood

- M-6: added an `@staticmethod clear_old_logs(days)` (using `frappe.db.delete`
  with a `modified < Now() - Interval(days)` filter) to the controllers for
  Operations Alert, Accommodation Occupancy Snapshot, and Vehicle Utilisation
  Snapshot, and registered them in `hooks.py` `default_log_clearing_doctypes`
  (Operations Alert 90 days; the two snapshots 365 days each). The financial
  ledgers (Accommodation / Fuel Consumption / Rental Accrual) are intentionally
  NOT registered — they carry audit significance and must persist.
- Test: new `test_log_clearing.py` (FrappeTestCase) proves the staticmethods
  exist, the hook is registered at the right retention with the ledgers excluded,
  and that clearing deletes only rows older than the retention window.

## If you are updating an existing site

- Run `bench migrate`, then `bench build` and `bench clear-cache`.
