## 1.7.0

_Transport requests now move through a real approval flow, and your reports answer more questions._

- **A guided approval flow for transport requests.** Each request now moves through clear steps with action buttons, and the right approver signs off at each level. A requester cannot approve their own request, so accountability stays clean.
- **The steps after approval now work.** Once a request is approved you can schedule it and mark it fulfilled, carrying it all the way to done instead of stalling.
- **Reports you can slice.** Cost and fleet reports gain Project, Company and Cost Center filters, so you can narrow a view to exactly the slice you need.
- **A chart at a glance.** The trend, aging and variance reports now show an inline chart alongside the numbers, so the shape of the data is visible without exporting.
- **Alerts that clear themselves.** Operational alerts close on their own once the issue behind them is resolved, so your list reflects what still needs attention.
