# Apex 1.37.1

A correctness fix for intercompany asset movements, plus a few Arabic labels.

## What changed for users

- **Intercompany transfers are now correctly gated.** When a Facility Asset
  Movement crosses companies, the release-approval / receiving-confirmation
  checks now fire reliably on the first save — previously the "is intercompany"
  flag was derived a step too late, so on a brand-new movement the gate could be
  evaluated before the flag was set.
- **More Arabic labels.** The Apex Core workspace card headings "Facilities",
  "Safety & Task Master", "Salis Fleet Master" and "Logs" are now translated.

## What changed under the hood

- `Facility Asset Movement`: moved `_populate_company_fields` + `_detect_intercompany`
  out of `before_save` and into `validate` (which runs first), and dropped the
  now-empty `before_save` hook. A regression test asserts validate derives
  `is_intercompany` and enforces the gate.
- Added the four card-break labels to `translations/ar.csv`.

## If you are updating an existing site

- Run `bench migrate`, then `bench build` and `bench clear-cache`.
