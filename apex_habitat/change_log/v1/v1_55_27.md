# Apex Habitat 1.55.27

A pre-arrival manifest web form for labour suppliers, a verified-list driver picker for fleet reassign, a daily open-alerts digest for supervisors, an operations-control compliance summary, and notification reliability hardening.

- Housing: labour suppliers can now submit a pre-arrival manifest from a public web form, listing the workers they expect to deliver to a building on a date — the accommodation team prepares beds and reconciles arrivals against it.
- Fleet: reassigning a vehicle now picks the new driver from a verified, permitted list instead of a free-typed id, so a typo can no longer silently mis-assign a vehicle.
- Fleet: supervisors now receive a daily digest of their open operations alerts, grouped by severity, alongside the existing per-alert critical notice.
- Fleet: the operations control board adds a compliance-at-risk count and a stopped-over-threshold count to its summary chips, scoped to your projects.
- Reliability: notification delivery is hardened so enabled alerts send dependably.
