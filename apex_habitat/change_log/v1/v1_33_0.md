# Apex 1.33.0

The arrival redesign's desk: a single-screen Arrivals Desk where a housing supervisor takes a worker from the building gate all the way to a bed, custody, an arrival card and transport — without leaving the page.

## What changed for users

- **Building-first desk.** Pick a building and its live rooms-and-beds floor-map appears beside a worker rail. Search a registered worker (Employee or existing Temporary Worker), or register a brand-new arrival by passport in the one pop-up the page has — a supervisor can create a Temporary Worker, never an Employee.
- **House with one click.** Click a free bed to house the selected worker. When a room is full, an "Over-capacity" button houses him in a clearly-marked temporary bed, capped by the building's overload limit. Everyone housed this session collects in an arrivals cart.
- **Custody, card and transport in side panels.** Hand custody to each housed Employee inline (a Temporary Worker's custody is deferred until he is linked); issue the Masar arrival link and print a welcome slip; and send several workers on ONE transport request — no dialogs, all on the one screen.

## What changed under the hood

- The Arrivals Desk page was rebuilt on the Frappe desk library as the "Anchor / Floor / Cart" board, reusing the Front Desk `get_building_grid` reader and the party-aware `quick_check_in`. New thin readers (`search_arrivals_workers`, `get_arrival_card`, `house_over_capacity`, `get_arrival_slip`) and the `register_temporary_worker` writer back it; over-capacity mints an `is_temporary` bed and lets the assignment enforce the building cap. Custody reuses `issue_cart`, the card reuses `issue_worker_link`, and transport reuses the Transport Request form — Temporary Workers board via the trip's unregistered manifest, not a new party field.

## If you are updating an existing site

- Run `bench migrate`, then `bench build` and `bench clear-cache`.
