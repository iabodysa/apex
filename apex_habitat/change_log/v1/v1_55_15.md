# Apex Habitat 1.55.15

Rent payment schedule cleanup — payment state tracked by status, the rent-due reminder simplified, plus a test-suite reliability fix.

- Rent Payment Schedule: dropped the never-populated payment-link field. Rent is paid externally, so the row Status (Unpaid / Paid / Overdue) is the single record of payment state.
- Rent Payment Due reminder: now triggers purely on an unpaid status, no longer also checking the removed link field.
- Test suite: hardened a transaction probe so its company and cost-center selection is deterministic, removing a cross-test state leak that made it flaky under different run orderings.
