# Apex 1.49.0

Two new cards on My Work show your submissions and what just closed.


- **A "Submitted By Me" card** shows the count of your still-open submitted documents at a glance, so you can see what you have in flight without opening a list.
- **An "Approved Last 48h" card** counts your documents that reached a final state in the last two days, giving you a quick read on what just wrapped up.
- **Both counts are strictly your own** — they reflect only what you submitted, never another person's documents, even if you can otherwise read everything.
- **They sit alongside the existing "Pending My Action" card**, so My Work gives you a fuller picture of where your work stands.

After updating, the two new cards appear on your My Work page.
