# Apex 1.49.0

My Work Center — the permission-safe worklist foundation (Path A).

## What changed for users

- **The My Work workspace gained two at-a-glance cards: "Submitted By Me"** (your
  still-open submitted documents) **and "Approved Last 48h"** (your documents that
  reached a final state in the last two days) — alongside the existing "Pending My
  Action" card.
- Both counts are **strictly your own**: they are computed per-user against your
  real permissions, so they never reveal another person's documents (a user who can
  read everything still sees only what they submitted).
- Under the hood, a new live worklist API (`get_my_work`) unions the four surfaces —
  awaiting your action, your open submissions, your recently-closed submissions, and
  your notifications — as the foundation for the full work-center page (next).

## If you are updating an existing site

- Run `bench migrate` to load the two new cards. No data change.
