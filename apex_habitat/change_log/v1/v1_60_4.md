# Apex Habitat 1.60.4

You can now run a two-sided bus boarding and departure flow, with live confirmation between the driver and each rider.

- **Live boarding manifest.** The driver sees every rider's status on the trip and confirms or rejects each boarding as it happens, so the bus only departs with the right people on board.
- **Themed boarding pass.** A rider opens a full-screen boarding pass on their phone, claims a seat, and asks the driver to wait a moment if they are close.
- **Departure countdown and wrong-bus help.** Riders get a clear countdown after each reminder, and anyone who scans the wrong bus is shown the correct trip and a way to reach the right driver.
- **Steadier reports and stronger checks.** Reports now show only the buildings and fleet you are allowed to see, and more fields are validated on entry, so existing data is unaffected and nothing changes retroactively.
