# Apex 1.38.0

Anyone can now report a maintenance issue, and new front-line roles give staff exactly the access they need.


- **Anyone can raise a Maintenance Request — and sees only their own.** Every signed-in person can report a maintenance issue without a special role, and a raiser sees only the tickets they opened — no one can browse another person's. The assigned technician sees the ticket too.
- **Oversight still sees and acts on everything.** Accommodation Manager, Resident Supervisor, Resident Request Coordinator, and System Manager continue to view and work every ticket, so nothing falls through.
- **Four new front-line roles.** Maintenance Technician, Cleaning Supervisor, Safety Officer, and Resident Request Coordinator give staff the access their job needs and nothing more, each grantable as a ready-made bundle in one step.
- **The Internal Auditor can review everything and change nothing.** Read and report access spans the custody, asset, movement, and ledger records, with no ability to create, edit, submit, or delete — oversight never alters a record.
- **Procurement and inventory keep their standard roles.** Stock and purchasing work continues on the built-in ERPNext roles, with no new role to learn.
