## 1.12.0

_Fuel claims and fuel exception cases now run on guided approval paths you can read at a glance._

- **Fuel claims follow a clear path.** A claim moves through **Submit to Movement, Reconcile, Approve, Close**, with a status you can read at a glance and a defined step for disputes. The person who raises a claim cannot approve their own.
- **Exception cases become a real investigation flow.** A case moves through **Open, Under Investigation, Evidence Required, Resolved or Rejected, Close** — and a case cannot be resolved until the required evidence is in, so issues are closed on the record, not from memory.
- **No more silent status edits.** Both a claim and a case change status only through their workflow buttons, so the status always reflects a real, permitted action by the right person.

After updating, claims and cases move only through their guided buttons.
