# Apex 1.38.1

Arabic translation polish on the desk workspaces.

## What changed for users

- **Two Apex Core workspace sections are now translated.** The "Setup & Master
  Data" and "Tools & Consoles" headings on the Apex Core workspace previously
  showed in English for Arabic users; both are now translated.
- **Consistent currency and unit labels.** Amounts in Saudi Riyal now read
  uniformly as "(ريال)" instead of the mix of "(ر.س)", "(ريال سعودي)", and
  "(بالريال السعودي)"; year units read as "(سنوات)". No numbers or fields
  changed — only the label wording.

## What changed under the hood

- `translations/ar.csv`: added the two missing Apex Core section headers and
  normalized 9 verbose/inconsistent parenthetical unit translations to the
  app's standard short forms.

## If you are updating an existing site

- Run `bench migrate`, then `bench build` and `bench clear-cache` to rebuild
  translations.
