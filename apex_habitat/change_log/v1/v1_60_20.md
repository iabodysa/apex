# Apex Habitat 1.60.20

Approvals now gate utility bills, leases, and deductions.

- **Utility bills get a second look.** A bill moves from draft to approval before it posts.
- **Leases need sign-off.** An accommodation lease is approved before it takes effect.
- **Deductions are reviewed.** An employee deduction acknowledgment now passes an HR approval step.
- Approvers cannot approve their own submissions; who can approve never changes.
