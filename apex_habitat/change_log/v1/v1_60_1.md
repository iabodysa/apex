# Apex Habitat 1.60.1

Security hardening and reliability fixes across portals, fleet, custody and reports.

- Tightened who can see what: asset, custody and driver records — and the cross-team reports and exports built on them — now stay scoped to your own organization, with no leakage across tenants.
- Made busy, concurrent actions safe: simultaneous stock issues, boarding scans, custody handovers and fuel-quota allocations no longer collide or double-count under load.
- Hardened the driver and worker portals: pages that wouldn't load now open reliably, and untrusted text on portal pages and printed slips can no longer inject scripts.
- Made background and billing jobs dependable: the fuel-accrual run always finishes and isolates a bad row, payroll deductions never apply twice, and utility bills reject negative or duplicate amounts.
- Closed assorted abuse paths and refreshed dependencies: rate-limited identity lookups, an allowlist for outbound web-push, updated app libraries with a CI lockfile check, plus faster hot paths and dead-code removal.
