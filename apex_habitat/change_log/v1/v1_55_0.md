# Apex Habitat 1.55

Portal experience release: clearer worker custody and transport, a reworked driver portal, RTL fixes, and workspace wiring.

## 1.55.34

_You can now keep the fleet board ahead of trouble, capture incidents and resident requests faster, and catch consumables before they lapse._

- **A live alert bell on the fleet board.** Watch the open-alert count update on its own, and from the drawer jump straight to the vehicle or the alert it belongs to.
- **The fleet board stays usable under partial failures.** If one data source hiccups the board still shows the rest instead of going blank, alongside a per-vehicle timeline and clearer workshop status.
- **Report a vehicle incident from a simple form.** A web form captures a fleet accident or theft for supervisor review before it takes effect.
- **A mobile triage list for resident requests.** Work the queue colour-coded by priority, advance a request's status with one tap, and mark several as triaged at once.
- **Custody warns before consumables lapse.** A daily check flags held items past their usable life so you can replace them before they fail.

Reliability and i18n improvements.

## 1.55.33

_A self-service glance for drivers and workers, custody you can audit end to end, and a report that surfaces residents everyone forgot._

- **Drivers get self-service.** In the driver app you now see your fuel quota and how much is left, your fuel-request history, and your recent trips alongside today's.
- **The worker app opens with a clear glance.** The home screen leads with your next ride, your open requests, and how many days until your Iqama expires.
- **Custody is auditable end to end.** Capture the recipient's signature at handover, and the right person now receives acknowledge-receipt and overdue-return alerts; a new view shows your top holders by custody value.
- **Forgotten residents surface on their own.** A report flags workers still housed after their project has ended, so a manager can act before the cost keeps bleeding.
- **Cleaning logs require photo evidence.** A cleaning round now needs its photos attached before it can be submitted.
- **Front Desk and the fleet board feel steadier.** Clearer permission and empty states, per-item progress as bulk actions run, and quicker filters help you find what you need.

Reliability and security improvements.

## 1.55.28

_A one-click departure-transport hand-off from a resident check-out, in-app alerts for drivers, a server-side fleet export, the Arrival Batch on the Housing workspace, and right-to-left polish on the Front Desk board._

- **Departure transport from a check-out.** When you complete a Final Exit or End of Contract check-out, an **Arrange Departure Transport** button raises the relocation transport for that worker in one step and links it back to the check-out. It only appears for those two reasons, and asking twice reuses the same request rather than creating a duplicate.
- **In-app alerts for drivers.** A driver is now notified in the app when their own clearance is blocked, and the current driver of a vehicle is alerted when that vehicle's compliance is expiring soon — alongside the existing alerts to fleet supervisors.
- **Fleet export of the full filtered list.** **Export CSV** on the Fleet Control board now builds the file on the server from your current filters, so it contains every vehicle you are allowed to see — not only the rows currently on screen.
- **Arrival Batch on the Housing workspace.** The pre-arrival manifest (Arrival Batch) now has its own link in the Housing workspace, so you can open it directly instead of searching for it.
- **Front Desk reads right-to-left.** The Front Desk bed board now mirrors cleanly in Arabic, with floor and bed headings shaped correctly instead of forced upper-case spacing.
- **Tidier asset-movement form.** The system section on the asset-movement form is now collapsed by default, keeping the everyday fields in view.
