# Apex Habitat 1.55.0

Portal experience release: clearer worker custody and transport, a reworked driver portal, RTL fixes, and workspace wiring.

- Worker custody now shows the receipt date, the issuing supervisor, and the building instead of a monetary value.
- Worker and driver trip views show project names instead of codes, plus an explicit no-route-planned state for trips without a plan.
- Driver portal defaults to Arabic, groups fuel and support requests under a My Requests section, and shows today's live attendance state.
- Fixed the fleet dashboard rendering mirror-flipped under right-to-left layout.
- Safety checklist portal is now iPad-friendly and follows the shared portal theme selector.
- Wired previously-orphaned reports and pages into their workspaces, each with an Arabic translation.
