## 1.15.0

_A new Apex Core workspace gives the shared settings a clear home in the desk._

- **One place for the shared settings.** Habitat Settings and Salis Settings, the configuration both modules read from, now live in their own Apex Core workspace, with shortcuts straight to each settings page.
- **Easier to find.** These settings previously had no workspace of their own; now there is an obvious home for them in the sidebar.
- **Open to the right people.** The workspace is available to the System Manager and to the Accommodation and Fleet managers who maintain these settings.

After updating, the new Apex Core workspace appears in your desk sidebar.
