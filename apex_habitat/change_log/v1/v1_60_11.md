# Apex Habitat 1.60.11

Field-level permission hardening across DocTypes

- Sensitive personal fields (passport, ID, nationality, phone, iqama) are now restricted to privileged roles via native field permission levels.
- Compensation and commercial-rate fields (deduction amounts, contract rates, retainers, service costs) are gated to finance and management roles.
- Token and OTP-hash fields are limited to privileged roles, with fleet desk access preserved for ad-hoc passenger details.
