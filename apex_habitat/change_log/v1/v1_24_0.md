# Apex 1.24.0

You can now issue a worker their personal link in one click — with a scannable QR and a WhatsApp share — right from their assignment.


- **Issue a link without leaving the assignment.** Open a worker's accommodation assignment and use the new **Issue Masar Link** button to get their personal link and a scannable QR on the spot — no separate record to open.
- **The QR always shows.** The code now renders reliably every time, so you can hand a worker a scannable link instead of typing it out.
- **Share over WhatsApp in one tap.** A **Send via WhatsApp** button opens a chat to the worker's registered number with the link ready to send — no WhatsApp setup needed, and it hides when no usable number is on file.
- **Clear on who can issue links.** Accommodation supervisors, HR, and fleet supervisors can issue worker links, so the right people can onboard without waiting on an administrator.

After updating, the Issue Masar Link button appears on the accommodation assignment form.
