# Apex Habitat 1.54.8

A new Fleet Control desk page gives fleet operations a live, filterable vehicle board.

- Fleet Control (opened from the Fleet Operations workspace) lists every vehicle as a status-coloured card or a table, with filters for status, rental office and project and a plate/category search.
- Selecting a vehicle opens a detail drawer with its driver, compliance, recent incidents and recent custody assignments, plus a link to the full record.
- The current view exports to CSV for offline sharing, mirroring the company's fleet worksheet.
