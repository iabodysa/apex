# Apex 1.29.0

Groundwork for onboarding workers who arrive before they exist in the system.

## What changed for users

- **New "Temporary Worker" record.** A worker arriving from the airport has only a
  passport (no Iqama yet). This new record captures them by passport number and is
  valid for 30 days, extendable to 90, while the Iqama is processed.
- **Nothing else yet.** This release only adds the record; the next steps will let
  housing and custody point at a Temporary Worker, and a scheduled task will link
  them to a permanent Employee once the Iqama issues (back-dating the cost).

## What changed under the hood

- New `Temporary Worker` DocType (Habitat module): passport (unique), nationality,
  project/supplier/building context, arrival date, a 30/90-day window with a
  computed expiry, a status (Active/Linked/Expired) and a `linked_employee` set
  later by the scheduler. Standalone — no other DocType changed in this batch.
