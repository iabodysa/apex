# Apex Habitat 1.29

You can now capture a worker who arrives before they have an Iqama.

## 1.29.1

_A new rent-due reminder, a room-label print on fresh sites, and a steadier worker-link dialog._

- **Get reminded when rent goes unpaid.** A new rent-payment-due reminder flags each unpaid rent row on its due date. It stays off by default — turn it on and set its email like the other reminders.
- **Print room labels on any site.** The accommodation room-label print is now available out of the box, including on a brand-new install.
- **Share a worker link the same way everywhere.** The share dialog — link, QR and WhatsApp — now behaves identically wherever you open it, whether from a worker token, an assignment or the arrivals desk.

After updating, the rent-payment-due reminder appears with your other reminders, off until you enable it.

## 1.29.0

_You can now capture a worker who arrives before they have an Iqama._

- **Record arrivals on the spot.** A new Temporary Worker record lets you log someone who lands with only a passport, by passport number, name, nationality and arrival date — no need to wait for an Iqama.
- **Track who they belong to.** Tie each arrival to its project, labour supplier, building and arrival batch, so you always know where a new worker sits.
- **See the temporary window at a glance.** Each record shows its window length and an expiry date, and carries a clear status — Active, Linked or Expired — that you can follow as the Iqama is processed.
- **Link to a permanent worker later.** When the Iqama issues, you can point the record at the matching employee, keeping the arrival history intact.

After updating, the new Temporary Worker record appears under Habitat.
