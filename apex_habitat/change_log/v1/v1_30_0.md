# Apex 1.30.0

You can now house and equip a newly arrived worker before their Iqama is issued — no need to wait for HR to register them first.


- **House a worker who has just arrived.** The accommodation assignment, check-out, room and bed transfer, resident request, idle-resident report, and the daily cost ledger now let you point a record at either a registered worker or a passport-only arrival, so a bed is ready from day one.
- **Nothing you already use changes.** Records for registered workers keep behaving exactly as before — the same lists, reports, and the daily accommodation-cost run stay intact.
- **Temporary arrivals stay cost-neutral until linked.** A passport-only worker is not charged on the daily cost run — nothing is deducted until they are later matched to a registered worker, at which point the cost back-dates automatically.
- **The What's New feed shows again.** Release notes once more appear in your inbox; long titles no longer block the whole batch from loading.
