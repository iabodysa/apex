# Apex 1.30.0

The records that track where a worker lives can now hold a worker who has arrived but is not yet a registered Employee — the second step of the arrival redesign.

## What changed for users

- **House and equip a worker before their Iqama is issued.** The accommodation assignment, checkout, room/bed transfer, resident request, idle-resident report, the daily cost ledger and the Masar worker link now each carry a **Resident Type** (Employee or Temporary Worker) and a **Resident** field, so a record can point at a permanent Employee or at a passport-only Temporary Worker.
- **Nothing you rely on changes.** The existing Employee field is kept and is filled in automatically from the Resident, so every list, report and the daily accommodation-cost run keep working exactly as before.
- **Temporary workers stay cost-neutral until linked.** A record for a Temporary Worker leaves the Employee field empty on purpose, so the daily cost allocation skips it until a later step links the issued Iqama to a real Employee and back-dates the cost.

## What changed under the hood

- Added a native `party_type` (Select: Employee | Temporary Worker) + `party` (Dynamic Link) pair to the seven worker-identity doctypes, mirroring the existing `item_type`/`item` pattern on Accommodation Stock Ledger.
- `employee` is now a read-only mirror kept in step by a shared `before_validate` helper (`apex_core/party_link.py`); a derived child (checkout, transfer) inherits its party from the parent assignment.
- An idempotent, existence-guarded backfill patch stamps `party_type=Employee`, `party=<employee>` on existing rows. It runs post-model-sync so the new columns exist first.
- Fixed the in-desk **What's New** feed: release titles are now clipped to Frappe's 140-char `Changelog Feed` limit. Long `1.x` titles were failing to insert and silently dropping the whole batch, so no What's-New item had appeared since the early releases.

- Run `bench migrate`, then `bench build` and `bench clear-cache`.
