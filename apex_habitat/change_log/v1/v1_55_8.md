# Safety operations: auto-remediation, same-day escalation, and coverage 1.55.8

The safety domain's live workflow now closes the loop: a failed check raises a tracked maintenance request, active hazards escalate the same day to the right managers, and daily/weekly checks surface overdue tasks and uninspected buildings to the Safety Officer. Seeded with real accommodation purchase items and a standard safety-task library.

- A failed safety check now automatically raises a tracked Maintenance Request and a structured finding, and notifies the facilities supervisor — no more manual follow-up
- An active safety hazard escalates the same day to the Admin Manager and Operations Director; a daily severity-aware overdue scan and a weekly all-building coverage check alert the Safety Officer; each supervisor now sees only their own buildings' safety records
- Seeded the items routinely bought for worker accommodation and a library of standard safety tasks, grounded in the operations records
- Fixed a defect where in-desk (system) notifications could fail to send
