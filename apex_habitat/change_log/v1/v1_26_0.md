# Apex Habitat 1.26

Reliability improvements in how fleet approvals are handled.

## 1.26.4

_A tidier sidebar with fewer, more logical workspaces._

- **Facilities in one place.** Maintenance and Safety now share a single Facilities workspace, since they are the same daily upkeep surface.
- **Movement in one place.** Transport and Fuel now share a single Movement workspace, matching how you actually work a dispatch and the fuel behind it.
- **Nothing else moved.** Fleet stays its own surface, and every report, page and record stays exactly where it was.

## 1.26.3

_A transport request now keeps worker trips and representative trips cleanly apart, so each is booked the right way._

- **Name the representative on a representative trip.** A transport request on the representatives line records which representative is travelling and can never be tied to labour accommodation or carry a worker manifest.
- **Keep worker trips for workers.** A bus or shuttle trip for workers cannot name a representative, and only these worker trips show up in the worker transport view.
- **Cleaner Employee form.** The fleet section on the employee record now reads simply as Salis, so it is easy to find at a glance.

After updating, the representative field is ready on new transport requests; existing trips keep working as before.

## 1.26.2

_A cleaner Salis settings screen._

- The old approval-request item and its unused settings fields are removed.
- Approvals work exactly as before, and a person still cannot approve their own document.
- Existing documents are unaffected; the cleanup applies from this update onward.

## 1.26.1

_A tidier Compliance workspace._

- The sponsorship-transfer record and its card are removed; the Compliance workspace reads cleaner.
- No cost or operations data is touched; only the unused government-relations item is gone.
- Existing records are unaffected; the change applies from this update onward.

## 1.26.0

_Reliability improvements in how fleet approvals are handled._

- The fuel and movement-cost documents now follow one consistent approval path.
- The same people approve as before, and no one can approve their own document.
- Nothing already approved is affected; the change applies to new documents going forward.
