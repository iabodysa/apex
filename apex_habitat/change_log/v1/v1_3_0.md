# Apex 1.3.0

You can now move around Salis faster and act right where you are.


- **Go straight to your area.** Salis is organised into clear areas — Workers Transport, Representatives Fleet, Fuel, Rentals and Costs, Compliance and Drivers, and Setup — so you open the part you work in and start there.
- **Create as you go.** Each area has one-tap create buttons — a new driver, vehicle, dispatch trip, fuel request or transport request — right where you need them.
- **Find your setup in one place.** Reference lists like vehicle categories, rental offices and fuel platforms, plus settings, now sit together under Setup.
- **Keep your access.** Roles have been tidied into a focused set that suits a small team while keeping approvals and oversight separated. Existing team members keep their access on upgrade.
