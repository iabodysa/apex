# Apex 1.4.0

You can now see Movement costs by Company and Cost Center, and slice the reports the same way.

## What changed

- **Company and Cost Center on Movement records.** Rental settlements, fuel claims, payment requests, and cost recoveries now carry a Company and Cost Center, filled in for you by default. Finance can group and filter these costs the way they do everywhere else.
- **Reports you can slice by Company.** The rental-cost, fuel-spend, cost-recovery, and payment reports gain a Company filter and column, so you can read each company's spend on its own.
- **Shorter, clearer area names.** Salis areas read as Workers, Fleet, Fuel, Rentals, Compliance, and Setup, so the sidebar is easy to scan with no cut-off names.
- **A faster way in.** A Help-menu shortcut takes you straight to Salis and the Dispatch Board.
- **Reporting only, never a ledger.** Movement still records what happened and posts no accounting entries; the Company and Cost Center are there for grouping and filtering, and your finance documents are produced on the finance side exactly as before.

After updating, the new Company and Cost Center fields fill in from your defaults, and existing records keep working.
