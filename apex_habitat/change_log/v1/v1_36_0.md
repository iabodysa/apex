# Apex 1.36.0

Reliability improvements to how the app's default records are kept in sync.

## What changed

- **Your tuned records are respected.** A default email template, task board, assignment rule, or maintenance entry you have edited keeps your changes.
- **Nothing is overwritten or duplicated.** New defaults are added only when missing; existing amounts, settings, and content are left exactly as they are.
- **Existing sites are unaffected retroactively.** This applies going forward only, and a record you deliberately changed is never reset.
