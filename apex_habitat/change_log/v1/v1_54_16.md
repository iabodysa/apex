# Apex Habitat 1.54.16

More Fleet Operations: a workshop-overstay metric and in-page quick actions.

- New "Workshop Overstay" number card on the Fleet Operations workspace counts vehicles still in the workshop past the overstay cutoff — the same rule as the Maintenance Overdue alert, scoped to your permitted projects.
- The Operations Control page (Fleet Control) vehicle drawer now has quick actions to open a new Vehicle Stop, Vehicle Incident or Vehicle Assignment, prefilled with the selected vehicle.
- Both reuse existing logic and the native submittable forms — no duplicate write paths.
