# Apex Habitat 1.54.7

A new Fleet Operations workspace brings the day-to-day fleet control surface together in one place.

- A public Fleet Operations workspace groups the live fleet metrics, the incident queue, custody actions and office-billing links for fleet supervisors and managers.
- New metrics surface open vehicle incidents and open theft reports at a glance, alongside the existing active, stopped, under-maintenance and active-driver counts.
- An Incidents by Type chart and an open-incident quick list make the current incident load visible directly from the workspace.
