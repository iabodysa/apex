## 1.46.0

_A Housing Supervisor now works only within the buildings they are responsible for._

- **Each supervisor stays in their own buildings.** Assign a supervisor to one or more buildings, and from then on they see and act only there — in lists, links, and lookups.
- **Scoped to the work they own.** Within their buildings a supervisor can house residents, issue custody from the store, and oversee cleaning; everything outside those buildings stays out of view.
- **One supervisor can cover several buildings**, so you can match real responsibility without splitting the role.
- **Oversight roles see everything as before.** Accommodation Manager, Internal Auditor, Finance Manager, and System Manager are not limited and continue to see every building.

After updating, assign each Housing Supervisor to the buildings they manage; until they have at least one, they will see no housing records.
