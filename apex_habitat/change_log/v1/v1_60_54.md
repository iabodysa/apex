# Apex Habitat 1.60.54

Building leases now go through a submit-for-approval step before they take effect.

- A lease starts as a draft and is sent for approval; it only takes effect once approved.
- The person who raises a lease cannot approve their own — a separate finance approver signs it off.
- Approved and active leases both count as in force across the lease reports, reminders, and dashboards.
