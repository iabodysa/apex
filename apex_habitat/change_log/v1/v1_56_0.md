# Apex Habitat 1.56

Operations and fleet boards now move in real time, custody runs from a scan-and-go kiosk, and drivers handle their own day from the app.

- **Live operations and fleet boards.** The Fleet Control and Operations Control boards now update on their own as vehicles and alerts change, so you see trouble as it happens instead of waiting for the next refresh.
- **A reworked arrivals, front desk, custody kiosk, and driver experience.** Tint arriving residents by project so same-project workers group together, open the bed board straight to your building, scan a worker badge or article barcode to hand custody out, and let drivers reply to tickets, report a problem, start a trip, and check their document expiries from the driver app.
- **Capture a signed acceptance at check-in.** A resident signs the housing terms on the arrivals screen, and the signature and the time are kept on their assignment for the record.
- **A guided custody go-live.** A step-by-step journey on the custody screen walks you through defining articles, issuing, returning, and assessing damage, so a new site can stand custody up in order.
- **The worker app reads fully in Arabic.** Status labels such as Left and Suspended now follow the language and no longer show in English, and a developer-mode demo seeds a sample worker-movement scenario so the worker and driver apps are not empty on first look.
- **What's New shows once per version.** The update note now appears once for a version instead of replaying the whole release history, alongside reliability work and tighter checks on who can perform each write action.
