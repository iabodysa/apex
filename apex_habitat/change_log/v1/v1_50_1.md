# Apex 1.50.1

Old non-financial depreciation snapshots are now tidied automatically.

## What changed for users

- The app now applies a **two-year retention** to **Non-Financial Depreciation
  Snapshots** (the operational asset book-value archives). A submitted snapshot
  older than 730 days is cleared by the daily housekeeping job together with its
  line items — so the table stays lean with no manual cleanup.
- This is strictly **non-financial** — it never touches any ledger. **Draft**
  snapshots are always preserved, and the financial ledgers are never auto-cleared.
- Reports keep two full years of snapshot history for asset-aging analysis.

## If you are updating an existing site

- Run `bench migrate`. Nothing is deleted on upgrade — the retention only applies
  to snapshots older than two years, going forward.
