# Apex 1.50.1

Old asset depreciation snapshots are tidied away on their own.

## What changed

- Submitted depreciation snapshots older than two years are cleared automatically, so the list stays lean without any manual cleanup.
- Nothing financial is touched: draft snapshots are always kept, and no ledger is ever changed.
- Existing data is untouched today; the two-year limit only applies to snapshots going forward.
