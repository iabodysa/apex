## 1.21.0

_A tidier settings home and at-a-glance numbers on every fleet workspace._

- **One clear place for settings.** The Apex Core workspace now leads with the four settings you actually reach for — Habitat, Salis, Integration, and the **driver-portal theme** — with the master-data clutter moved back to where that data lives.
- **Live KPI tiles on every fleet workspace.** Fleet, Fuel, Driver Compliance, Rentals & Costs, Representatives, and Workers Transport each open with quick tiles — open requests, active drivers and vehicles, licences expiring soon, settlements pending, litres dispensed, and more.
- **A new training guide.** A walkthrough of who can use each screen and how, so a new supervisor or front-desk user can get going without a hand-over session.
