# Apex 1.18.2

Your vehicle, driver and passenger-manifest lists now show only your own projects.

## What changed

- **Lists respect your project.** A supervisor scoped to a project sees only that project's vehicles, drivers and passenger manifests, not every project's.
- **Your own access stays open.** A driver still sees their own record, and Fleet Managers, Finance and auditors still see everything.
- **Existing records are untouched.** The scoping applies going forward; nothing already saved is changed.
