# Apex Habitat 1.45

Reliability improvements in fleet and movement records.

## 1.45.10

_Clearer Arabic wording and tidier workspace titles._

- **Arabic reads more naturally.** Wording across the desk was refined to clearer standard Arabic, and the wizard Next and Back arrows now point the right way for right-to-left reading.
- **Workspace section titles translate correctly.** Titles like Residents and Rooms and Costs and Settlement now show in Arabic, and the redundant "(Habitat)" no longer appears on the settings label.
- **Your data is unaffected.** Only labels and translations changed; existing records stay exactly as they were.

## 1.45.9

_Each guided setup step now explains itself, so you know what it does and why before you start._

- **Clearer guidance at every step.** Each onboarding step now opens with a short intro that tells you what it sets up and why it matters, instead of a bare one-line title.
- **Covers both setup flows.** The fuller guidance appears across accommodation and safety setup as well as fleet and movement setup.
- **Easier first run.** A new user can follow the setup in order and understand each step's place in the workflow without asking around.

## 1.45.8

_Reliability fixes in the Movement KPI report, with more of the desk reading in Arabic._

- **Your Movement KPI Summary now counts the full month** you select, so fuel-consumption figures no longer come up short.
- **Amounts and totals stay exactly as recorded** — only the date range the report reads from is corrected, and more labels and status options now read in Arabic.
- **Nothing already recorded is changed** — the correction applies the next time you open the report; past entries are untouched.

## 1.45.7

_Bug fixes and stability improvements._

- **The Unresolved Alerts card files correctly.** It now groups under Habitat when you pick cards, instead of appearing with no home.
- **Behaviour stays the same.** No screen, total or approval changes; this is housekeeping only.
- **Existing data is unaffected.** Nothing on your site is altered by this release.

## 1.45.6

_The My Work entry in the sidebar now shows its icon._

- **My Work has its sidebar icon back.** The entry previously showed blank and now displays correctly.
- **Nothing else changes.** The page content and its links stay exactly as before.
- **Existing data is unaffected.** This is a display fix only and touches none of your records.

After updating, refresh the Desk once if the old blank icon still lingers.

## 1.45.5

_Reliability fix for the Action Inbox._

- **The Action Inbox opens cleanly.** A leftover approval from a removed record type no longer causes an error, and stale leftovers are tidied away on their own.
- **Your real work is safe.** Genuine pending approvals and tasks are untouched; only broken leftovers are removed.
- **Existing data is unaffected.** The cleanup applies going forward and changes nothing about your active items.

## 1.45.4

_The full set of Movement dashboards now appears on a brand-new site._

- **Every Movement dashboard is present from the start.** The Workers Transport view and its tiles now appear on a new install, not only after a later update.
- **Nothing changes for existing sites.** Your dashboards and any edits you made stay exactly as they are.
- **No data is affected.** This adjusts only how a fresh install sets up its dashboards.

## 1.45.3

_The Movement dashboards are ready the moment Apex is installed._

- **Movement dashboards show up right away.** The fleet, finance and operations views appear on a new site without waiting for a later update.
- **Your tiles stay yours.** Vehicle, fuel, trip and driver cards are unchanged, and any view you have customised is left exactly as set.
- **Existing sites are untouched.** This only closes a gap on fresh installs; nothing on a running site is altered.

## 1.45.2

_Reliability improvements in how starter data is set up._

- **Setup is more consistent.** The default assignment rules, email templates and boards you start with on a new site are provisioned the same way every time.
- **Your edits are always preserved.** The safety task catalog and any changes you have made to starter records are never overwritten on update.
- **Existing data is unaffected.** This applies to fresh setup only; nothing already recorded is touched.

## 1.45.1

_More of the desk now reads in Arabic._

- **Arabic is more complete.** The action inbox, arrivals and front desks, custody kiosk, transfer board, safety map, dispatch board and fuel console now show in Arabic, along with on-screen validation and error messages.
- **Only the wording changed.** Every screen works exactly as before — labels and messages, nothing else.
- **Your data is unaffected.** Existing records are unchanged by this update.

_Reliability improvements in fleet and movement records._

- **Fleet and movement screens behave the same.** Handover, write-off, cost transfer and vehicle stop continue to work exactly as you expect.
- **Your records and approvals are unchanged.** Who can submit, what gets validated, and your existing data all stay the same.
