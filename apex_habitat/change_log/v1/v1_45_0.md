# Apex 1.45.0

Reliability improvements in fleet and movement records.

## What changed

- **Fleet and movement screens behave the same.** Handover, write-off, cost transfer and vehicle stop continue to work exactly as you expect.
- **Your records and approvals are unchanged.** Who can submit, what gets validated, and your existing data all stay the same.
- **Existing data is unaffected.** Nothing already recorded is altered by this update.
