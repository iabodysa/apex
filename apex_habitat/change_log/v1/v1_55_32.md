# Apex Habitat 1.55.32

You can now reassign a vehicle's driver in one step, identify a worker by scanning their Iqama or personal link at check-in, and act on several vehicles at once from the fleet board.

- **Reassign a vehicle's driver in one step** — from the fleet control drawer, pick the new driver and the previous assignment closes automatically.
- **Identify a worker by scanning their Iqama or personal link** at check-in — matched in one tap, and anyone who already holds a bed is flagged before you assign one.
- **Act on several vehicles at once** — select multiple vehicles on the fleet board and stop them or send them to the workshop in a single action.
