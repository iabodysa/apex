## 1.2.0

_A new Dispatch Board, ready-to-hand paperwork, sharper reports, and alerts that chase the things that used to slip._

- **See the whole fleet at a glance.** The Dispatch Board shows vehicles by status, today's trips, and which drivers are free versus already out, so you can assign the next job in seconds.
- **Print the paperwork your team hands over.** Produce clean documents to hand over a vehicle, settle a rental, pay a fuel claim, or clear a driver.
- **Reports that answer real questions.** Check vehicle utilisation, fuel spend by vehicle, rental cost by office, transport fulfilment, and cost-recovery aging.
- **Salis chases what slips.** It raises alerts for expiring vehicle compliance, blocked driver clearances, and overdue fuel requests, and routes new transport requests and tickets to the right owner.
- **Find your way by area.** Movement is organised into spaces for Workers Transport, Representatives Fleet, Fuel, Rentals & Costs, Compliance & Drivers, and Setup.
- **Set your own approval thresholds.** The authority tiers and the amounts that trigger an Operations sign-off are yours to adjust in settings as the company grows — change them whenever you need.
