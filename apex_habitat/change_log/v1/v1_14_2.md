# Apex 1.14.2

A redesigned, phone-friendly driver portal and a clearer fuel approval board.


- **A driver portal built for your phone.** A clean home screen shows your status, assigned vehicle, and licence at a glance, with the licence turning amber and then red as it nears or passes expiry, plus large, tap-friendly action tiles.
- **A clearer Fuel Approval Console.** Each pending request now leads with the driver name and vehicle plate instead of a code, shows litres and amount up front, and flags over-threshold requests and sorts them first. Approve and reject still work exactly as before, and amounts are never changed for you.
- **A friendlier landing for office staff.** Opening the portal as office staff now shows a tidy card with quick links to your desk tools, rather than a plain list.

After updating, open the driver portal once on your phone to see the new home screen.
