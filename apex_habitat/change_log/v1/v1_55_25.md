# Apex Habitat 1.55.25

Fleet compliance-risk badges on the control board, building-scoped access to resident requests and idle reports, vehicle-theft and workshop-overstay alerts, a pre-arrival manifest, and a driver monthly-attendance view.

- Fleet: each vehicle card on the control board now flags a compliance risk, showing whether its next document expires soon or has already expired and by how long.
- Housing: building-scoped supervisors now see only their own estate's resident requests and idle reports, matching the scope already applied to assignments and custody.
- Fleet: reporting a vehicle theft and a vehicle overstaying in the workshop now raise alerts to the fleet and government-relations teams.
- Housing: a new pre-arrival manifest (Arrival Batch) records the workers a supplier expects to deliver to a building on a date, and feeds arrival-reconciliation and completion tracking.
- Fleet: drivers can now see their own attendance for the month in the driver portal, with each day's status and stamped times.
