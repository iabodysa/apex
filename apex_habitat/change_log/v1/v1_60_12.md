# Apex Habitat 1.60.12

A mobile Housing Inventory count portal for field supervisors, and asset custody sign-off now moves the custodian.

- **Count from your phone.** A new inventory count portal lets a supervisor pick a building, count each item by room, and submit, scoped to their own estate.
- **Variance for you.** Each count shows counted against expected automatically.
- **Custody moves on sign-off.** Asset custody sign-off now updates the custodian, and restores the prior one if the assignment is cancelled.
