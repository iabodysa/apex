# Apex 1.13.0

Dispatch trips now run on a clear path, and the whole Movement suite is guided end to end.


- **A clear path for every trip.** A dispatch trip moves through **Planned, Dispatched, Completed**, with a status you can read at a glance.
- **Completing a trip does the right things for you.** Marking a trip Completed fulfils its linked transport request and advances the vehicle's odometer in one step — no separate manual updates.
- **Calling off a completed trip is clean.** Cancelling a completed trip reverses those effects: the transport request returns to Scheduled and the trip's posting is undone, so nothing is left half-done.
- **The whole Movement suite is guided.** Transport requests, settlements, clearances, payments, tickets, transfers, fuel requests, claims and exceptions — and now dispatch trips — all change status only through guided, permitted actions by the right person.

After updating, dispatch trips move only through their guided buttons.
