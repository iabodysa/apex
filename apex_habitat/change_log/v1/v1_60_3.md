# Apex Habitat 1.60.3

Your driver and safety screens now update themselves, and the worker boarding pass shows just the trip a worker needs.

- Live updates: the driver and safety screens refresh on their own the moment a trip or safety round changes, and the worker portal quietly refreshes itself whenever it's the screen you're looking at.
- Cleaner worker boarding pass: redesigned as a themed ticket that shows the worker only their own pickup and destination, not the full driver route.
- Better directions: opening a stop in Maps now drops you on the real place, with matching sample data so demos line up.
- Driver portal: trip cards now fit neatly on small phone screens.
- Safety reports are grouped into five clear sections on the workspace.
- More of the app reads correctly in Arabic.
