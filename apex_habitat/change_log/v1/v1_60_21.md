# Apex Habitat 1.60.21

Approvals for contracts and damages, plus a cleaning audit report.

- **Contracts get sign-off.** A subcontractor service contract is approved before it becomes active.
- **Damage charges are reviewed.** A custody damage assessment needs supervisor approval before any deduction.
- **See cleaning at a glance.** A new housing cleaning audit report shows cleaner, approval, and photo evidence, filtered by date, building, and status.
- Approvers cannot approve their own submissions; access stays scoped to your buildings.
