# Apex Habitat 1.55.24

Resident-request and trip notifications, a one-time supervisor backfill, a Front Desk open-requests badge, a fleet release-vehicle action, recover now closes the theft report, and a one-click damage assessment from a custody return.

- Housing: a resident request being resolved now notifies its coordinator and the assignee, so a closed request no longer goes unnoticed.
- Fleet: scheduling a trip now notifies the dispatch desk and the requester, keeping both informed the moment a trip is booked.
- Housing: a one-time backfill fills in the responsible building supervisor on older accommodation assignments that pre-date the field, so the expected-checkout reminder reaches the right supervisor for them.
- Housing: the Front Desk bed board now shows a badge of a building's open resident requests that clicks through to the filtered list.
- Fleet: the Fleet Control drawer can now release a stopped vehicle back to service in one action, closing its open stop through the native lifecycle.
- Fleet: recovering a vehicle now closes the open theft report it had on record, instead of leaving it open forever.
- Housing: a submitted custody return with damaged or lost items now offers a one-click "Create Damage Assessment" that carries those items into a pre-filled draft.
