# Apex Habitat 1.55.16

Custody serialized-article enforcement, a new occupancy metric card, and form-layout and portal polish.

- Custody: a serialized article is now enforced as one tracked unit per line — it must carry a serial number and a quantity of exactly one, on both issue and return.
- Housing: a new Buildings Over Threshold metric card counts buildings whose occupancy exceeds their own over-capacity threshold, surfaced on the Housing workspace.
- Forms: system and reversal-status fields on three records are isolated into their own collapsible sections, keeping the working fields uncluttered.
- Worker portal (Masar): the hero artwork now positions correctly under right-to-left using a logical CSS property.
- Driver portal: corrected the Arabic afternoon greeting.
