# Apex 1.8.0

Rental settlements and driver clearances now move through a guided approval flow.


- **A clear path for rental settlements.** A settlement now moves Reconcile, then Approve, then Mark Paid, so everyone can see exactly where it stands.
- **Only Finance can mark a settlement paid.** The "Mark Paid" step is reserved for Finance, and no one can approve their own settlement, keeping the money sign-off in the right hands.
- **A clearance you cannot rush.** A driver can be marked Cleared only once fuel, custody and vehicle returns are settled. Until then the flow does not offer the step, so nothing slips through.
- **The steps after approval now work.** Once a document is approved you can carry it through its remaining steps to a clean finish.
