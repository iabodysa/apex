# Apex 1.23.0

A worker can now open one personal page to see their accommodation, transport, and documents — and raise a request.

## What changed

- **A self-service page for each worker.** Opening their personal link shows four areas: **My Profile** with documents and iqama / passport expiry warnings, **My Accommodation** with their building, room, bed, and the in-charge contact, **My Transport** with pickup point and time, route stops, and the assigned vehicle and driver, and **My Requests** to raise a maintenance, transport, or HR request and follow its status. It opens in Arabic and switches to English.
- **No account to manage in the field.** Each worker signs in through a private personal link you can print as a QR — there is no username or password to hand out or reset.
- **The driver route view now lives with the driver.** The "my route today" screen moved into the **My Route** area of the driver portal, where drivers already work.
- **A new Cost by Dimension report.** See accommodation cost broken down by company, building, and project, with a total row — read-only, drawn from your existing records.

After updating, generate a worker's link from their assignment and share the link or QR with them.
