# Apex 1.25.0

You can now log accommodation safety incidents in one place, and the serious ones raise the alarm for you.


- **Record any safety incident.** Capture the date, building and exact location, the incident type (fire, electrical, injury, structural, health and hygiene, security, or other), the number of people hurt, what happened, and the immediate action taken — then close it later with resolution notes.
- **Serious incidents alert management on their own.** When you submit an incident marked severe or critical, management is notified with a direct link to the record, so nothing serious gets chased by hand.
- **Severity is always recorded.** The severity field starts blank and must be chosen, so an incident can never slip through filed as low by accident.
- **Resident links stay protected.** A worker's accommodation link stops working once that worker is marked left or inactive, so an old link can never expose current information.

After updating, the new safety incident record is ready to use; to receive the management alerts, ask your administrator to switch them on.
