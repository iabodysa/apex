# Apex Habitat 1.59

Drivers can board a trip even when a pass won't scan, Masar opens the whole route in Maps, and you can send a worker their personal link straight to their phone.

## 1.59.1

Reliability fixes across driver attendance, fuel and rental movement, and maintenance.

- Driver hours, fuel allocation, rental returns and maintenance close-out now record accurately and stay in step across the two fleet screens.
- Amounts, ledgers and who can approve are unchanged; existing records keep their values.
- The fixes apply going forward and disturb nothing already recorded.

- **Board a trip without scanning.** When a boarding pass won't scan, the driver ticks each worker who got on from the trip's passenger list and confirms — the boarded count and the manifest stay in step, and re-confirming the same worker changes nothing.
- **See boarded against expected at a glance.** Each trip now shows how many riders are aboard versus how many were expected, and the driver checks off each stop as the route is completed.
- **Open the whole route in Maps.** From Masar a worker opens the full pickup route in Google Maps with every stop chained in order, so the drive follows one tap instead of stop-by-stop.
- **Send a worker their Masar link to their phone.** From the arrivals screen, send a worker their personal Masar link over WhatsApp or SMS in one tap — off until you set up a gateway in Settings, and it only sends a link a worker already has.
- **Fill arrivals from the passport.** On the arrivals screen you can scan a passport to pre-fill the worker's details for you to check — opt-in in Settings, and you can always type everything in by hand.
- **Know when a new version is ready.** The worker and driver apps now show a small banner when an update is available, so a tap loads the latest version.
