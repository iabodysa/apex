# Apex Habitat 1.6

Shared settings now live together in one place.

## 1.6.1

_A cleaner desk that opens straight to your Apex areas._

- **A focused sidebar.** Your desk now leads with Habitat, Salis and Apex Core, with the stock background areas tucked out of the way so the everyday menu stays short.
- **Nothing is removed.** The tidy-up only hides; every area can be switched back on whenever you want it, and your records are untouched.
- **Existing setups are unaffected.** The change applies going forward and does not move or delete anything you already use.

## 1.6.0

_Shared settings now live together in one place._

- Habitat Settings and Salis Settings now sit together under a new Apex Core area, so they are easier to find.
- What each setting does is unchanged, and every saved value carries over.
- Nothing is reset; your existing configuration stays exactly as it was.
