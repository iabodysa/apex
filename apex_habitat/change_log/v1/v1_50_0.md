# Apex 1.50.0

Your work inbox is now a full My Work Center — one screen for everything that's yours.


- **One screen for all your work.** My Work Center brings together your pending approvals, your assigned tasks, your open submissions, what closed in the last 48 hours, and your notifications — each in its own section.
- **Approve or reject without leaving the page.** Pending approvals show the available actions inline, so you can move a document forward in one click; the approval still runs through the standard Apex workflow, so who can approve never changes.
- **Close an assigned task in place**, or jump straight to any document from its row — every section links you right to the record.
- **Everything shown is strictly yours.** Each section reflects only your own documents and assignments, never another person's, even if you can otherwise read everything.

After updating, the page is titled My Work Center under My Work.
