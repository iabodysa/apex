# Apex 1.45.9

The guided onboarding steps now explain themselves.

## What changed for users

- **Every onboarding step now has a short intro** that explains what the step does and why it matters
  in the workflow — across both the Habitat (accommodation/safety) and Salis (fleet/movement) setup
  flows. Previously the steps showed only a one-line description with no fuller guidance.

## What changed under the hood

- Populated `intro_text` on all 17 Onboarding Steps (it was empty on every step). Content only — no
  behavior, schema, or permission change.

## If you are updating an existing site

- Run `bench migrate` to refresh the onboarding steps. Nothing else to do.
