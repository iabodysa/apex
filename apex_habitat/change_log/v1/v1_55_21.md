# Apex Habitat 1.55.21

A restyled safety map page, a resilient fleet board, a manager arrival-summary endpoint, broader safety-round reminders, and a cost-bleed-by-building chart.

- Safety: the safety map desk page is restyled to match the portal design, so it reads consistently with the rest of the experience.
- Fleet: the fleet control board now shows an error message with a Retry action when it cannot load, instead of silently blanking the screen.
- Housing: a new read-only arrival-summary endpoint gives managers today's housed count, a by-supplier breakdown, and over-capacity placements.
- Safety: the no-recent-safety-round reminder now also reaches the building's own responsible supervisor, not just the safety officer.
- Housing: a new Cost Bleed by Building chart visualizes estimated accommodation cost bleed grouped by building.
