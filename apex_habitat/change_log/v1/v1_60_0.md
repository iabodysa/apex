# Apex Habitat 1.60.0

Keep a running count of what's in each building, and let drivers get a push even when the app is closed.

- **Count what's in every building.** A new housing inventory register tracks countable items — furniture, appliances, linen, kitchenware — per building and room, with what you expect against what was actually counted, the difference, and each item's condition.
- **See shortfalls and worn items at a glance.** Each count shows the variance from the expected quantity and flags items that need attention, so a missing or damaged item surfaces on its own.
- **Maintenance keeps the count honest.** When a work order on a linked item is completed, the matching inventory row updates itself — the maintenance date moves forward and a "needs maintenance" flag clears — so you never re-key it by hand.
- **Drivers can get a push with the app closed.** A driver can opt in on their device to receive background notifications, so a trip assignment or a fuel decision reaches them even when the app is not open. Off until you turn it on and set it up in Settings; nothing is sent until then.
