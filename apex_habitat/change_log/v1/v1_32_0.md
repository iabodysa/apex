# Apex 1.32.0

Apex now links a temporary arrival to their registered Employee record automatically once HR registers the matching passport — you no longer re-key the worker by hand.

## What changed

- **Automatic linking by passport.** Each day, Apex matches an active temporary arrival to a registered Employee carrying the same passport number. On a match it joins them, moves every housing and custody record over to the Employee, and marks the arrival as linked.
- **Skipped cost catches up on its own.** The accommodation cost that was paused while the worker was on a passport is back-dated to their registered record from the check-in date, so nothing is missed and nothing is double-counted.
- **Lapsed arrivals are flagged for HR.** A temporary worker whose window passes without a passport match is marked expired, and HR receives an in-app notice to follow up.
