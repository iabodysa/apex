# Apex 1.20.0

Each driver can switch the portal to their own language and see their own profile and vehicle.

## What changed

- **Read the portal in your language.** A new English/Arabic toggle in the header switches the whole portal between the two languages, with full right-to-left layout for Arabic. English stays the default, and your choice is remembered on your device.
- **Open your own driver profile.** A driver can see their name, status, phone, project, employee link, and licence number with its expiry — with a colour warning when the licence is close to expiring.
- **See your assigned vehicle.** A vehicle page shows the plate, category, status, ownership, and the date the vehicle was assigned, with a clear message when none is assigned yet.
- **You only ever see your own details.** The profile and vehicle pages show the signed-in driver's own data, nothing belonging to anyone else.

After updating, the language toggle, profile, and vehicle pages appear in the driver portal.
