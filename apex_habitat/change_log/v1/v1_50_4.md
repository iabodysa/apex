# Apex 1.50.4

You can now reach your own My Work page no matter your role.


- **Open to everyone signed in.** My Work is no longer limited to a fixed set of roles, so anyone with work waiting can open it.
- **Only your own work.** You still see just your pending approvals, assigned tasks, open submissions, recently closed items, and notifications, all on one screen.
- **A cleaner, personal view.** The old shared task list that mixed in everyone's tasks is gone; what is left is the work that belongs to you.

After updating, My Work opens for every signed-in user.
