# Apex Habitat 1.61.0

Workers can now rate their trips from the Masar portal.

- Every completed trip in the Masar portal shows a "Rate your trip" card until it is rated.
- Workers can give a 1-to-5 star rating and add optional feedback in a few taps.
- Once submitted, the trip card updates to a "Rated" checkmark and the rating is recorded against the trip for fleet review.
