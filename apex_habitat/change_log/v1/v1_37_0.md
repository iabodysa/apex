# Apex Habitat 1.37

A new Pending My Action card shows, at a glance, how many documents are waiting on you.

## 1.37.1

_Reliability fix for asset movements that cross between companies._

- **Cross-company movements are gated correctly.** When an asset movement crosses companies, the release-approval and receiving-confirmation checks now apply reliably from the first save.
- **The approvals themselves are unchanged.** Who must release and who must confirm a transfer stays exactly as before.
- **Existing movements are unaffected.** The fix applies to new and re-saved movements going forward; records already processed are untouched.

## 1.37.0

_A new Pending My Action card shows, at a glance, how many documents are waiting on you._

- **Your own live count.** A **Pending My Action** card sits at the top of the Apex Core workspace under My Work, showing how many documents need you right now — the approvals you can act on plus the tasks assigned to you.
- **One step to act on them.** The card is the number; tap through to the **Action Inbox** to Approve or Reject in place. Together they let you see and clear your work without searching.
- **Always only your work.** The count is figured per person from the same sources as your inbox, so everyone sees just their own pending items.

After updating, the Pending My Action card appears under My Work.
