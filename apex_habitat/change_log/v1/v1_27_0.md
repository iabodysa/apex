# Apex Habitat 1.27

A new Arrivals Desk lets you onboard arriving workers from one screen, reception style.

## 1.27.1

_Three things that were built but hard to reach are now linked where you expect them._

- **Safety getting-started is back.** The Safety onboarding steps now appear again on the Facilities workspace, so new users get guided through that surface.
- **Cost by Dimension is one click away.** The report is now linked in the Costs workspace instead of being reachable only by direct address.
- **Arrivals Desk has a shortcut.** A shortcut to the new Arrivals Desk now sits in the Accommodation workspace, alongside the rest of housing.

## 1.27.0

_A new Arrivals Desk lets you onboard arriving workers from one screen, reception style._

- **Onboard from a single screen.** Pick the arriving workers, see each identity card — photo, project, current bed, custody and link status — and finish the whole intake without jumping between forms.
- **House, issue, link, dispatch in order.** Assign a building, room and bed, hand over custody items, create the worker access QR, and request transport, all from the same desk.
- **One QR for the whole group.** Generate the access QR for every not-yet-issued worker at once, then print an arrival slip to hand over on the spot.
- **You only see what you can do.** Any step you are not allowed to perform stays disabled with a reason, so the desk never opens a path you do not already have.

After updating, the Arrivals Desk appears as a shortcut in the Accommodation workspace.
