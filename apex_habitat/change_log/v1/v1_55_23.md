# Apex Habitat 1.55.23

Acknowledge and resolve actions on the operations alert queue, one-tap room readiness on the bed board, clearer guidance when no buildings are in scope, checkout that reflects its damage deduction, and Employee-only Masar worker links.

- Fleet: operations alerts can now be acknowledged and resolved directly from the control board, advancing each alert through its status ladder with an audit trail.
- Housing: the Front Desk bed board now offers a one-tap "Mark Ready" on rooms that need attention, so staff can clear a room without leaving the board.
- Housing: the Housing Supervisor report now explains when no buildings are in the viewer's scope and how to request access, instead of showing a blank grid.
- Housing: a checkout now reflects the damage deduction posted by its custody assessment, linking the deduction and its amount back onto the checkout's financials.
- Masar: a worker self-service link can now only be issued to a permanent Employee, with a clear message that the link becomes available once the worker's Employee record exists.
