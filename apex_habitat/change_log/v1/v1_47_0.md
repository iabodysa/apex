# Apex Habitat 1.47

A new setup step lets you choose your key Apex settings the first time you set up a site.

## 1.47.7

_Security and reliability improvements._

- Behind-the-scenes housekeeping keeps Apex steady, with no change to any screen you use.
- Your data, amounts, and approvals stay exactly as they were — nothing is altered.
- Existing records are untouched; the tidy-up applies to how Apex runs, not to your work.

## 1.47.6

_Bug fixes and stability improvements._

- An old, unused setup checklist that no longer appeared anywhere has been removed for a cleaner Setup area.
- The live Accommodation Go-Live and Safety Readiness checklists are unchanged and still guide your setup.
- Nothing is altered retroactively. Your existing setup and its progress are untouched.

## 1.47.5

_Bug fixes and stability improvements._

- Updates to buildings, sites, and the Habitat workspace now carry through reliably when you upgrade.
- The way these records look and behave on screen is unchanged by design.
- Nothing is altered retroactively. Existing buildings, sites, and the workspace stay as they are.

## 1.47.4

_The Checkout Pending Clearance report now shows the resident's name next to each ID._

- **See who each clearance is for** — the Checkout Pending Clearance report gains an Employee Name column, so you can follow up by person instead of reading a raw ID.
- **Consistent with the resident register** — names now appear here the same way they already do in the Active Resident Register, so the two reports read alike.
- **Same list, same totals** — filters, totals, and which checkouts appear are unchanged. Only the new name column is added.

## 1.47.3

_Bug fixes and stability improvements._

- Beds, rooms, cities, fuel logs, drivers, vehicles, and trip records show their correct numbering rule in their settings.
- Document IDs are generated exactly as before, and nothing about how records are named has changed for you.
- Nothing is altered retroactively. Existing records keep their current IDs.

## 1.47.2

_Security and reliability improvements._

- Sensitive actions on a building, work order, or scheduled task are checked against the exact record you are working on, so access stays tight.
- Who can approve, create, or change records is unchanged by design, and system-generated records are still produced the same way.
- Nothing is altered retroactively. Existing records and their permissions are untouched.

## 1.47.1

_A label fix so one more option reads in Arabic._

- **One theme option now shows its Arabic label** instead of staying in Latin script.
- **Nothing else changes** — only the displayed text is affected, not any setting or behaviour.
- **Existing data is untouched** — the corrected label appears going forward.

## 1.47.0

_A new setup step lets you choose your key Apex settings the first time you set up a site._

- **Pick your defaults up front.** When you set up a fresh site, a new Accommodation & Fleet step lets you choose the default payment method before going live.
- **Salary deduction stays off by default.** You decide whether the housing allowance is deducted from salary — nothing is deducted unless you turn it on.
- **Custody and ledger postings stay off by default.** Choosing to recover custody damage or to post accommodation costs to the General Ledger is your call, and both start switched off.
- **Safe when prerequisites are missing.** If you enable a deduction or ledger posting before its accounts and approver are set, the wizard keeps your payment method, leaves that toggle off, and tells you to switch it on later — it never starts deducting silently.

You can change any of these choices later in Habitat Settings; the setup step runs only once, on a brand-new site.
