# Apex 1.47.0

A first-install Setup Wizard step, so a new site can choose its key Apex settings up front.

## What changed for users

- **A new "Apex — Accommodation & Fleet Setup" step appears in the setup wizard on a fresh site.**
  It lets the operator choose, before going live:
  - the **default payment method** (Payment Entry / Payment Order / Expense Request),
  - whether to **deduct the housing allowance** from salary (**off by default — nothing is deducted**),
  - whether to deduct custody damage and whether to **post accommodation costs to the General Ledger**
    (both **off by default**).
- Everything can still be changed any time afterwards in Habitat Settings; the wizard runs once.
- **Safe by design:** if you choose to enable a deduction or GL posting but its prerequisites (an
  authorizer, accounts) are not configured yet, the wizard saves your payment method and leaves the
  toggle off, telling you to enable it later in Habitat Settings — it never starts deducting silently.

## What changed under the hood

- Native Frappe setup-wizard integration: `setup_wizard_requires` registers the slide
  (`public/js/apex_setup_wizard.js`); `setup_wizard_complete` →
  `apex_core.setup_wizard.setup_wizard_complete` applies the choices to Habitat Settings, with a
  validation-safe fallback (covered by `tests/test_setup_wizard.py`).

## If you are updating an existing site

- Run `bench migrate` + `bench build`. (The wizard slide only appears on a brand-new site's first
  setup; existing sites keep configuring via Habitat Settings.)
