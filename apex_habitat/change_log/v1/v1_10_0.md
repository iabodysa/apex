# Apex 1.10.0

Everything about fuel now lives on one screen — a single Fuel Request for ordering, top-ups, and chips.

## What changed

- **One Fuel Request for everything.** Ordering fuel, topping up a vehicle's quota, and issuing, replacing or cancelling a fuel chip used to be three separate forms. Pick the type at the top of one Fuel Request and only the fields that matter appear.
- **Temporary top-ups roll back on their due date.** When you grant a temporary quota top-up with a due date, it reverts automatically on that date, so a vehicle's quota does not quietly creep upward over time.
- **One list, one set of filters, one approval path.** All fuel activity sits in a single place instead of three, so there is less to learn and less to hunt for.
- **Your existing requests carry over.** Past top-ups and chip records move into the unified Fuel Request for you — nothing is lost and no re-entry is needed.

After updating, all fuel activity appears under the single Fuel Request list.
