# Apex 1.50.2

Two reliability fixes in the resident check-out and custody-damage flow.

## What changed for users

- When you check a resident out with **damaged or lost custody items**, the app
  auto-creates a draft **Custody Damage Assessment**. It now always takes the
  **Building** from the resident's housing assignment, so the draft is created
  reliably — previously, if the bed record had no building set, the draft could
  be dropped silently and the damaged items left without an assessment.
- When **damage deduction** is enabled, submitting a Custody Damage Assessment
  raises a draft payroll deduction on the worker. A non-HR user — for example a
  **Housing Supervisor** — can now submit the assessment without being blocked;
  the deduction is raised on their behalf and still arrives as a **reviewable
  draft** for HR, fully logged and linked back to the assessment.
- No change to amounts, ledgers, or who approves the deduction — only who is
  able to record the assessment.

## If you are updating an existing site

- Run `bench migrate`. Nothing is changed retroactively — the fixes apply to new
  check-outs and assessments going forward.
