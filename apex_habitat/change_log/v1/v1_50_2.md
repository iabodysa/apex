# Apex 1.50.2

Reliability fixes in the resident check-out and custody-damage flow.

## What changed

- Checking a resident out with damaged or lost items now reliably opens a draft damage assessment.
- Amounts, ledgers, and who approves a payroll deduction stay exactly the same; deductions still arrive as a reviewable draft for HR.
- Nothing changes retroactively; the fixes apply to new check-outs going forward.
