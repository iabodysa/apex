# Apex Habitat 1.1

## 1.1.1

_Security and reliability improvements._

- **Steadier day to day.** Routine screens and saved records behave more reliably.
- **Nothing changes in your data.** Your existing records, amounts, and approvals stay exactly as they were.
- **Applies going forward.** This is a maintenance update only — nothing is altered retroactively.

## 1.1.0

_Salis now opens straight to the numbers that matter, on a cleaner two-division workspace, with status colours you can read at a glance._

- **Know where things stand before you click.** The Salis workspace leads with a Key Metrics row — active vehicles, vehicles in the shop, open transport requests and pending approvals — so the picture is there the moment it opens.
- **Go straight to the work you do.** The workspace is organised around its two service lines, a Workers Transport section and a Representatives Fleet section.
- **Get the full picture from two dashboards.** Workers Transport shows request status and type, transport over time, and demand by project; Representatives Fleet shows vehicles by status, fuel spend trends, rental accrual and dispatch trips.
- **Read status by colour everywhere.** Vehicles, drivers, trips, fuel, maintenance and accommodation now wear colour-coded status in lists, on Kanban and Calendar boards, and in reports — and the colour always follows the record's own status.
