# Apex Habitat 1.54.29

A smarter, faster Safety Checklist — it shows only what's due and emails the manager.

- The Safety Checklist now shows only the rounds that are due right now — daily, weekly, monthly and so on appear automatically from what was last done, so the supervisor never picks a cadence by hand.
- Each task is checked with one tap (pass, fail, or issue) plus an optional note, and the whole visit is submitted in one step on a new mobile-friendly screen at /safety.
- When a round is submitted, a report is emailed to the manager — the recipient is configurable in Habitat Settings, otherwise it goes to the Accommodation Manager.
