# Apex 1.28.3

A security fix for cross-project access on two fleet records.

## What changed for users

- **Project boundary now holds on direct access.** A fleet supervisor scoped to
  one project could previously open or fetch (by direct URL / API) a Salis Driver
  or Passenger Manifest belonging to another project, even though the list views
  correctly hid them. That gap is closed — direct record access is now
  project-scoped like the lists.
- **Drivers keep their own profile.** A driver can still read their own Salis
  Driver record.

## What changed under the hood

- Added the missing `has_permission` hooks for Salis Driver and Passenger Manifest
  (the query-condition and document-permission lists are now symmetric), plus a
  driver-aware `salis_driver_has_permission` that mirrors the list query's
  owner-OR-project logic so self-profile reads are not over-restricted. Access was
  only tightened; oversight roles and segregation-of-duties are unchanged.
