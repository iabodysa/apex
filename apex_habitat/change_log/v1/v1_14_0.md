## 1.14.2

_A redesigned, phone-friendly driver portal and a clearer fuel approval board._

- **A driver portal built for your phone.** A clean home screen shows your status, assigned vehicle, and licence at a glance, with the licence turning amber and then red as it nears or passes expiry, plus large, tap-friendly action tiles.
- **A clearer Fuel Approval Console.** Each pending request now leads with the driver name and vehicle plate instead of a code, shows litres and amount up front, and flags over-threshold requests and sorts them first. Approve and reject still work exactly as before, and amounts are never changed for you.
- **A friendlier landing for office staff.** Opening the portal as office staff now shows a tidy card with quick links to your desk tools, rather than a plain list.

After updating, open the driver portal once on your phone to see the new home screen.

## 1.14.1

_Reliability fixes in the driver portal._

- The driver portal now opens reliably for signed-in drivers and staff.
- Approvals, amounts, and who signs off stay exactly as before.
- The fix applies going forward; your existing records are unchanged.

## 1.14.0

_Your alerts list now clears itself when the cause is fixed, and keeps a record of how each one closed._

- **Alerts close on their own when the situation is resolved.** When a licence is renewed, a vehicle is back in use, or a temporary fuel top-up is reverted, the matching alert clears automatically, so your list reflects what still needs attention.
- **Excessive top-up alerts clear too.** The one alert that used to linger now goes away once the temporary top-up is reverted or the vehicle's consumption is back within its quota.
- **A resolution trail you can read back.** Each cleared alert now records when it closed and why, so you can see how an issue was handled instead of guessing.
- **Less standing noise.** Your open alerts show only the items that genuinely still need action.
