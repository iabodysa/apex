# Apex 1.14.0

Your alerts list now clears itself when the cause is fixed, and keeps a record of how each one closed.


- **Alerts close on their own when the situation is resolved.** When a licence is renewed, a vehicle is back in use, or a temporary fuel top-up is reverted, the matching alert clears automatically, so your list reflects what still needs attention.
- **Excessive top-up alerts clear too.** The one alert that used to linger now goes away once the temporary top-up is reverted or the vehicle's consumption is back within its quota.
- **A resolution trail you can read back.** Each cleared alert now records when it closed and why, so you can see how an issue was handled instead of guessing.
- **Less standing noise.** Your open alerts show only the items that genuinely still need action.
