## 1.41.0

_Reliability and access fixes across custody, worker links, and snapshots._

- **Custody damage deductions never apply silently wrong** — if a payroll
  deduction cannot be raised, the assessment stops instead of finishing with
  nothing deducted; amounts and approvers are unchanged.
- **Linking a temporary worker stays put** even if its note fails, and snapshots
  stay read-only for the internal auditor.
- **Your existing records are untouched** — this applies going forward only.
