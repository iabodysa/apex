# Apex Habitat 1.18

You can now tie worker pickups to where people live and record who actually boarded each trip.

## 1.18.4

_A driver's portal check-in now counts as attendance on its own, with nothing left half-recorded._

- **Checking in records attendance for you.** When a driver checks in from the portal, their attendance is logged right away, so the day is accounted for without anyone re-entering it.
- **A "missing attendance" alert clears itself.** The moment a driver checks in, any open reminder that they had not yet been marked present resolves on its own.
- **Check-out completes the same day, not a second one.** Checking out finishes the existing record with worked hours, time, and photos, so each driver shows one clean entry per day instead of a leftover draft.

## 1.18.3

_Cleaner Arabic labels across the app._

- **Standard terms read more consistently.** Common terms like statuses and month names now use Frappe's own Arabic wording instead of a duplicate copy.
- **Your domain terms stay translated.** Every accommodation and movement term remains fully translated, and nothing falls back to English.
- **Nothing you entered is affected.** The change touches labels only; your records and data are unchanged.

## 1.18.2

_Your vehicle, driver and passenger-manifest lists now show only your own projects._

- **Lists respect your project.** A supervisor scoped to a project sees only that project's vehicles, drivers and passenger manifests, not every project's.
- **Your own access stays open.** A driver still sees their own record, and Fleet Managers, Finance and auditors still see everything.
- **Existing records are untouched.** The scoping applies going forward; nothing already saved is changed.

## 1.18.1

_Reliability fix in the rental settlement payment flow._

- **A payment request is raised only on an approved settlement.** A contested or not-yet-approved settlement can no longer start a payment.
- **Amounts and approvals are unchanged.** The normal path — reconcile, approve, then request payment — works exactly as before.
- **Nothing already in flight is disturbed.** The check applies going forward; existing settlements and payments are untouched.

## 1.18.0

_You can now tie worker pickups to where people live and record who actually boarded each trip._

- **Pickups tied to housing.** A route stop can now point to a worker's accommodation building, so a pickup is linked to where workers actually live, not just a place on a map.
- **A boarding record for every trip.** A new Trip Start Log captures how a trip ran — who boarded, at which stop, by QR scan or manual check-in — and counts expected against boarded so you can see who is missing.
- **Contractors and temporary hires included.** A boarding record can list unregistered riders alongside registered workers, so a trip's count is complete even when the people on board are not all on file.
- **A driver's whole day in one view.** A driver can pull today's worker route — its stops in order, each stop's housing pickup, and the rider list — scoped to their own identity, so one person only ever sees their own day.
