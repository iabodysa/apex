# Apex 1.45.3

The Movement dashboards are ready the moment Apex is installed.


- **Movement dashboards show up right away.** The fleet, finance and operations views appear on a new site without waiting for a later update.
- **Your tiles stay yours.** Vehicle, fuel, trip and driver cards are unchanged, and any view you have customised is left exactly as set.
- **Existing sites are untouched.** This only closes a gap on fresh installs; nothing on a running site is altered.
