# Apex 1.0.0

A whole new Movement module, Salis, joins Habitat — so you now manage where your workforce lives and how it moves in one place.


- **Run your fleet and transport from a new workspace.** Salis covers two service lines: shuttling workers from camp to project site and relocating them between cities, plus administrative and document-signing trips for representatives with rider cars, fuel, and vehicle handover and return.
- **Let approvals route themselves.** Every fuel, transport and transfer request finds the right approver on its own — small ones clear fast, and bigger ones (cross-project, over-threshold, large inter-city moves, frequent admin trips) escalate up the chain, with only the right person able to sign them off.
- **Stay on top of fuel.** Set quotas, raise and top up requests, track chips, follow a daily consumption ledger, reconcile each month, and settle disputed fuel with an evidence-backed exception case. Clear pending requests from the Fuel Approval Console. These are operational records and never post to a finance ledger on their own.
- **Track rental cost from receive to settlement.** Receive and return rental vehicles, watch the daily rental cost build up, and close the month with a single Rental Settlement that hands finance a ready-to-pay request. Cost recovery and cost transfer come along as operational memos that reference your finance documents.
- **Give drivers a portal in their pocket.** A mobile-first web app at **/driver** lets a driver check in and out, see today's trips, request fuel, and raise a support ticket — each driver sees only their own.
- **See compliance and people at a glance.** Track each vehicle's registration, insurance, inspection and operating card with expiry alerts, capture signed handover evidence, and clear every driver before dispatch. Open an Employee or a Supplier and their Movement story — driver record, cost recovery, rental office — is right there as quick links.
