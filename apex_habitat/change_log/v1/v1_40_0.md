# Apex Habitat 1.40

A new "My Work" home opens on what is waiting for you.

## 1.40.2

_Reliability improvements to your dashboards and nightly cost runs._

- **Dashboards and cards stay reliable** as the app updates over time.
- **Nightly cost allocation runs once per building**, with no double-counting,
  and amounts are unchanged.
- **Your existing data is untouched** — this applies going forward only.

## 1.40.1

_Your My Work page is now a real personal hub, not just a counter._

- **One place to start your day.** My Work brings your pending actions, your
  maintenance requests, and your personal status cards together, so you land on
  a page that shows what is waiting on you.
- **Jump straight to your Action Inbox.** A shortcut on the page takes you to
  everything pending your action, without hunting through menus.
- **See your maintenance requests at a glance.** A My Maintenance Requests
  shortcut opens the requests you raised, so you can track where each one stands.
- **Watch what you have moving.** Cards show what is pending your action, what
  you submitted, and what was approved in the last 48 hours.

After updating, the fuller My Work page appears the next time you open it.

## 1.40.0

_A new "My Work" home opens on what is waiting for you._

- **A My Work home near the top.** A new workspace called My Work sits near the top of the sidebar and opens on the things waiting on you.
- **Your pending count, front and center.** A live Pending My Action count shows how many items need you right now.
- **One click to act.** A shortcut takes you straight into the Action Inbox, where you Approve or Reject.

After updating, My Work appears near the top of your sidebar.
