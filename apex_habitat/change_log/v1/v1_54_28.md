# Apex Habitat 1.54.28

The Safety Checklist page — run a building's safety round in one screen.

- New Safety Checklist page: pick a building and a cadence, tick off that cadence's safety tasks, and submit the whole round in one step — it records the round and each task result together and shows the overall result.
- The page is open to the supervisor roles that can sign off a round (Accommodation Manager, Resident Supervisor, System Manager) and is linked from the Safety workspace.
- The old Safety Inspection Report is now fully retired from the desk: its number card and trend chart read the new Safety Rounds, and the safety onboarding step opens the new page.
