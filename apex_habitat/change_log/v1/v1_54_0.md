# Apex 1.54.0

Six business features: financial integration, salary-deduction layer, cost-field privacy, safety auto-tickets, rider guard, and model cleanup.

- Financial integration is now live: a finance-approved payment request becomes a real payment document (Frappe's native Payment Request by default), and General Ledger posting is gated by the enable-GL-posting switch.
- New policy-driven salary-deduction layer (skeleton): a deduction policy plus per-type rules (damage, rent, fuel, custody) and an employee consent record, with Saudi-labour-law caps and NO deduction until explicitly activated.
- Sensitive cost figures are now hidden from non-finance roles via field-level permissions, scoped to finance and the Accommodation Manager role.
- Safety inspection findings now auto-generate Maintenance Requests (one per actionable finding, idempotent).
- A rider on leave or marked inactive can no longer be issued a vehicle or fuel; instead a clearance task is opened for the movement supervisor to recover the vehicle and custody.
- Removed two unused dead fields as part of the model-integrity cleanup.
