# Apex Habitat 1.54.18

Two fleet alert windows are now configurable in Salis Settings.

- Workshop Overstay Days (default 14) — how long a vehicle may sit in the workshop before a Maintenance Overdue alert.
- License Alert Lead Days (default 30) — lead time for driver licence-expiry alerts.
- Both were already read by the alert jobs, but the fields were missing from the form, so they were stuck at their defaults; now they can be set.
