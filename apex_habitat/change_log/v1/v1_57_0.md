# Apex Habitat 1.57

Drivers now run their whole day from a phone-friendly app, and every workspace opens straight to the screen you actually use.

- **A driver app you can install and use offline.** Drivers add the app to their home screen and keep working when the signal drops, with a clear banner showing when they are seeing saved data.
- **Everything a driver needs in one place.** Snap a shift photo at check-in, open a trip or vehicle in Google Maps with one tap, follow a fuel request and its decision, and check license, clearance, and vehicle-compliance dates before they lapse.
- **Drivers hear about what matters.** A driver is notified about a trip set for tomorrow and about a fuel request decision, in the app and by their usual channel, so nothing is missed.
- **Register a driver without leaving the flow.** A new driver form lets a supervisor add a driver on the spot before assigning a vehicle, and a vehicle handover can be captured during a reassignment — saved as a draft your manager reviews and approves.
- **Leaner workspaces across the app.** Each workspace now opens with the portal or screen you use most and a short, curated set of shortcuts and counters, with the clutter of extra charts and lists cleared away.
- **A clearer first-time fleet setup.** The fleet workspace now surfaces the fuel setup step, and number counters render correctly across workspaces.
