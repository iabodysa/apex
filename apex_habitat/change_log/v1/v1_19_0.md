# Apex 1.19.0

You can now give the driver portal your own look, and the desk reads fully in Arabic.

## What changed

- **Choose how the driver portal looks.** Pick a theme from the portal settings — the company brand, the standard light look, or dark — and optionally set an accent colour and logo, so the portal matches your team.
- **The portal stays in English on purpose.** Because the driver workforce is multilingual, the portal keeps one shared language everyone can follow, while the look is yours to set.
- **The desk now reads in Arabic throughout.** Onboarding, reports, charts, cards, dashboards, workspaces, and settings that were still showing English are now translated, so the whole desk feels consistent.
- **The Habitat space is easy to find again.** Its name now shows clearly instead of looking like a generic system page, so you can spot it at a glance in the sidebar.
