# Apex 1.34.0

The Arrivals Desk, reworked from live-desk feedback: it now looks and behaves like a stock Frappe page, spreads across the full screen, hands over custody like a store, and prints signed paperwork with a QR.

## What changed for users

- **Native look, full-width layout.** The desk dropped its custom colours for standard Frappe styling and now lays out across three columns — worker intake on the left, the live floor-map in the centre, the session actions on the right — instead of one crowded side panel. The building picker no longer hides behind the header.
- **Custody like a store.** Each worker's custody is now a multi-item cart: add several articles and quantities (with live store balances) and hand them all over at once, in one issue.
- **One QR for the group, signed paperwork.** A single button issues the Masar QR for every housed worker at once — shown inline, no pop-up. Printing now produces real forms to sign: a check-in slip with housing terms, a custody handover slip listing the issued items, and an arrival card carrying the worker's QR.

## What changed under the hood

- The Arrivals Desk page was rebuilt on native Frappe desk styling (indicator pills, theme tokens, in-body `make_control` anchors — which also fixed the header covering the building picker). New endpoints: `batch_issue_worker_links` (group QR), `get_checkin_slip`, `get_custody_handover_slip`; `get_arrival_slip` embeds the Masar QR via the now-public `masar_qr_data_uri`; custody reuses `get_kiosk_catalog` + `issue_cart`. No new Print Format doctype — the slips are server-rendered Jinja print views.

## If you are updating an existing site

- Run `bench migrate`, then `bench build` and `bench clear-cache`.
