# Apex 1.34.0

The Arrivals Desk now spreads across the full screen, hands over custody like a store counter, and prints signed paperwork with a QR.

## What changed

- **Full-width, familiar layout.** The desk now lays out across three clear columns — worker intake on the left, the live floor map in the centre, and the session actions on the right — instead of one crowded panel. The building picker is no longer hidden behind the header.
- **Custody like a store counter.** Each worker's custody is now a multi-item cart: add several articles and quantities, see live store balances, and hand them all over together in a single issue.
- **One QR for the whole group, signed paperwork.** A single button issues the arrival QR for every housed worker at once, shown right on the screen. Printing now produces real forms to sign — a check-in slip with housing terms, a custody handover slip listing the issued items, and an arrival card carrying the worker's QR.
