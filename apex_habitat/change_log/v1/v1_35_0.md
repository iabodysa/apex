# Apex 1.35.0

You now have one Action Inbox that shows only the documents waiting on you, with Approve and Reject right there.

## What changed

- **One place for everything awaiting you.** The new **Action Inbox** gathers, just for you, the approvals you can act on and the tasks assigned to you — so you stop hunting across separate lists. Anything already approved or already moved on never appears.
- **Approve or reject without opening the document.** Each item shows the actions actually available to you, and you act on them **right from the inbox**. Your authority is checked every time, including any self-approval limits, so nothing slips through.
- **The list stays honest.** The inbox **keeps itself current** and quietly clears out stale rows left behind when a document changed elsewhere, so what you see is always real work.

After updating, the Action Inbox appears under Apex Core.
