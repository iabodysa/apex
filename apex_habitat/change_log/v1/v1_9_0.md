# Apex Habitat 1.9

Payment requests and support tickets now move through a guided approval flow.

## 1.9.1

_A new manager view in the driver portal, and a home screen that opens reliably for everyone._

- **A manager panel in the portal.** Open the portal while signed in as staff rather than a driver and you now get a friendly panel with quick links to your Salis workspace and the Dispatch Board, instead of a dead end.
- **The driver home screen opens reliably.** Drivers reach their home screen cleanly again, with no blank page on the way in.
- **Honest errors with a way forward.** If the portal genuinely cannot load, it now says so and offers a Retry button, rather than wrongly claiming your account is not linked.

After updating, do one hard refresh in the browser so the portal picks up the new version.

## 1.9.0

_Payment requests and support tickets now move through a guided approval flow._

- **A clear path for payment requests.** A request moves from draft, to submitted to Finance, to approved, to paid, so its status is never a guess.
- **Finance holds the money steps.** Only Finance approves a payment request and marks it paid, and no one can approve their own request. Movement still posts no accounting entries, so nothing touches a ledger.
- **Support tickets follow a status flow.** Tickets move through a clear set of states as staff pick them up, resolve them and close them, and a driver still sees only their own tickets.
- **The steps after approval now work.** Once a document is approved you can carry it through its remaining steps to a clean finish.
