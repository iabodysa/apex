# Apex 1.9.0

Payment requests and support tickets now move through a guided approval flow.

## What changed

- **A clear path for payment requests.** A request moves from draft, to submitted to Finance, to approved, to paid, so its status is never a guess.
- **Finance holds the money steps.** Only Finance approves a payment request and marks it paid, and no one can approve their own request. Movement still posts no accounting entries, so nothing touches a ledger.
- **Support tickets follow a status flow.** Tickets move through a clear set of states as staff pick them up, resolve them and close them, and a driver still sees only their own tickets.
- **The steps after approval now work.** Once a document is approved you can carry it through its remaining steps to a clean finish.
