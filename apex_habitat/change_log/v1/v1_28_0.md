# Apex Habitat 1.28

Driver support tickets now run on the standard Issue desk, with response and resolution targets.

## 1.28.3

_Project boundaries now hold for fleet driver and passenger manifest records._

- A fleet supervisor scoped to one project can no longer open a driver or passenger manifest from another project, even by a direct link.
- A driver can still see their own profile, and oversight roles and approvals stay exactly as they were.
- The change applies going forward and disturbs no existing records.

## 1.28.2

_Reliability improvements to how your operational notifications install._

- The full set of operational reminders — license and lease expiry, maintenance, resident requests, safety incidents, fleet compliance, overdue fuel and the rest — installs more reliably on a fresh or updated site.
- Every reminder stays off by default and unchanged; you still turn on only the ones you want, exactly as before.
- Existing sites are unaffected — any reminders you already enabled keep their settings.

## 1.28.1

_The support-ticket SLA now pauses on the right business holidays._

- **Timers use your real calendar.** Ticket response and resolution targets now follow the company holiday list, so they pause on the days you are actually closed.
- **Setup stays safe.** If no holiday calendar is configured yet, tickets keep working normally until an admin sets one.
- **Nothing already in progress is disturbed.** Existing tickets and any SLA already in place are unaffected; the corrected calendar applies going forward.

## 1.28.0

_Driver support tickets now run on the standard Issue desk, with response and resolution targets._

- **Tickets gain a real SLA.** Each ticket now carries response and resolution targets and a full conversation timeline, so you can see at a glance which ones are running late.
- **The driver experience is unchanged.** A driver raises and follows up on a ticket from the portal exactly as before — only the desk side behind it is now the standard Issue tooling.
- **Your categories carry over.** Vehicle, Fuel, Attendance, Salary and Other stay as the ticket categories, and Low, Medium, High and Urgent stay as the priorities.
- **Right people, right access.** A driver sees only the tickets they raised; fleet and project managers can read and work them; finance and audit can read.
